# Artifacts — Talismans


|  | Available only with the All Under Heaven DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Talismans are trinket artifacts that are obtained by  Mandala rulers each time they start a great project to upgrade their realm capital to the final level. The artifact has **Common** rarity and gives **+2** Monthly Piety. If the Bejeweled Reliefs contribution is paid for, when the great project is finished the rarity of the artifact obtained from it will be increased to **Famed** and it will gain an additional bonus based on the owner's Mandala Aspect.

| Mandala aspect | Contribution effects |
| --- | --- |
| Creation | **+15%** Fertility **Small Boost** to Health |
| Serenity | **+2** Vassal Limit **-12%** Short Reign Duration |
| Destruction | **+6** Natural Dread **+5** Controlled Territory Defender Advantage **+15%** Pursuit Efficiency |
| Trickery | **+8** Days Enemy Hostile Scheme Phase Length **Small Boost** to Disease Resistance |
