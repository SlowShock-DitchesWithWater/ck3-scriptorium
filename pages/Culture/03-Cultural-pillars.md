# Culture — Cultural pillars


Each culture has 5 cultural pillars: Heritage, Language, Martial Custom, Aesthetics, and Ethos.

- Heritage represents a culture's origin and prevents creating hybrid cultures if both cultures share it.
- Language represents the language that all characters and counties of a culture speak, and determines the title naming and what the culture's knights are called. If a character converts to a culture with a different language, they will instantly learn it.
- Aesthetics determine the naming practices as well as the appearance of architecture, armies, clothes and coat of arms.
- Martial Custom determines what genders can act as Commanders and Knights and requires the Royal Court DLC. Culture heads can only change it to something that matches their gender succession law. Without it the faith's View on Gender doctrine determines it.

#### Reform Ethos

|  | Available only with the Royal Court DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

A line graph showing size penalty

A culture's ethos can be replaced with another, this costs 20000  Prestige, but the time it will take to establish depends on the number of counties the culture is present in:

- If the culture is present in ≤5 counties the reformation will take 5 years **(no penalty)**.
- If the culture is present in 6-29 counties the reformation will take the same amount of time as the # of counties. Example: 16 counties = 16 years **(scaling penalty)**.
- If the culture is present in ≥30 counties the reformation will take 30 years **(flat penalty)**.

Only the previous ethos bonuses will apply during the reforming period. There is no cooldown after reformation, so a new ethos can be reformed to immediately afterward.
