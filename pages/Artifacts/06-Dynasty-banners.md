# Artifacts — Dynasty banners


|  | Available only with the Royal Court DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Dynasty banners are large wall ornament artifacts that are given to the first Dynasty Head of each dynasty who obtains a Royal Court. If the ruler loses all it's  Kingdom or higher titles the character who took the last title will get the artifact but if they're not part of the same dynasty only the Court Grandeur bonus will be given. The rarity and effects of dynasty banners increase with the dynasty's Level of Splendor.

| Rarity | Monthly Renown | Monthly Prestige | Court Grandeur | Level of Splendor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Common** | 0.02 - 0.04 | 0.04 - 0.08 | 0-1 | Base origins - Noteworthy |
| **Masterwork** | 0.06 - 0.08 | 0.12 - 0.16 | 2 | Reputable - Significant |
| **Famed** | 0.10 - 0.12 | 0.20 - 0.24 | 3 | Famous - Fabled |
| **Illustrious** | 0.14 - 0.16 | 0.28 - 0.32 | 4 | Legendary |
