# Attributes — Obedience


|  | Available only with the Khans of the Steppe DLC *or* the All Under Heaven DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

*(Only Rulers and their courtiers of Meritocratic Khanate and  Nomadic governments)*

Vassals and their courtiers have an Obedience value, which determines whether they will be  Obedient or  Disobedient.  Obedient vassals will not create or join factions against their liege, will not start hostile schemes against their liege and will migrate with their liege,  Obedient Kurultai are necessary to have a safe succession, and  Obedient tributaries can have Tributary War Support in their contract. Obedience can be ensured for 15 years by paying  Herd,  Gold,  Dread, or a  Hook.

How much Obedience everyone needs to be obedient depends on the liege's title rank. In employing a  Chief Law Overseer the amount is reduced by 1-5 depending on the courtier's Aptitude.

| Title rank | Obedience to be Obedient |
| --- | --- |
| Count | 50 |
| Duke | 70 |
| King | 100 |
| Emperor or Hegemon | 150 |

Obedience is calculated the following way:

| Positive obedience | Negative obedience |
| --- | --- |
| **always obedient** if character is a herder or received a Paiza artifact **+0.5** per Dread if it's above 10 and higher than the target's **+1** per Opinion **+25** if Legitimacy is Rightful, True or Ordained **+20** if Golden Lieneage **+35** if Conqueror **+50** if Greatest of Khans **+50** if having a truce **+100** if fulfilled a Purge Pretenders oath **+10** from extended family **+15** from employed courtiers **+25** from Stargazer **+25** from kurultai (**50** with Honored to Serve perk) **+25** from close family **+25** from consorts/concubines **+50** from children **+50** from guardians **+50** from wards **+50** from spouses (**+75** with Loyalty and Respect perk) **+50** from Chief Law Overseer **+100** from friends **+100** from child vassals **+125** from child courtiers **+250** from primary heir **+1000** from best friend **+1250** from blood sibling | **-10** if having less Dread **-15** if having less Prowess **-15** if Legitimacy is Illegitimate, Aspiring or Recognized **-50** if no special relationship **-850** if lost a was with the Humiliation War casus belli within the last 5 years **-15** from unemployed courtiers **-100** from heirs who aren't Preferred Successor and gender law allows them to inherit **-100** from rivals **-500** from characters with more Herd **-500** from characters with more military strength **-1000** from nemeses |
