**Medieval Arts** (or **MA**) is a large cosmetic pack for Crusader Kings III which brings the greatest monuments, natural wonders and cities of the medieval world to life. It was first published in April 2021 with the objective of fleshing out the arts and cultures of the map through an exploration of styles, Ruins and the Art School, but has since narrowed its scope to improving the visual aspects of the map through hand-made 3D models. Its design philosophy is simplicity: its script and content are intentionally kept at a manageable size, and the mod best works as a seamless addition to the game, only noticed as one zooms closer into the map.

#### Content

MA adds over **100** new unique, hand-made 3D map objects. The vast majority of these models are introduced as Special buildings, each with its own unique modifiers, history and flavor text, but some are map objects without any "interactable" content, primarily the case for unique cities.

New content is regularly introduced as part of seasons through updates (large) and patches (small), often with a regional focus. Currently, the mod is in **season 3.5**, prioritizing improvements to core regions of the map and older content.

#### Compatibility

MA is designed with compatibility in mind and can be used with virtually any other mod on the Workshop. It is part of the **Community Mods for Historicity** (CMH) playset and a Representative of the **CK3 Mod Co-op** in order to maintain active discussions with other graphical mods. Potential compatibility issues - due to design limitations of the game engine - are visual glitches tied to locators, but they are generally very rare and not intrusive, and can be easily fixed by placing MA lower in the load order.

The mod is both **ironman** and **achievement compatible** .

#### Translations

MA is primarily written in English, but it supports a **full translation** in all of the game's official langagues. These include:

- French
- German
- Japanese
- Korean
- Polish
- Russian
- Simplified Chinese
- Spanish

The translations are all machine-made. They were initially checked and approved by native speakers, but since then rely on volunteers pointing out potential issues.
