# Medieval Arts — Map objects


Part of Medieval Arts' content is introduced exclusively for visual purposes, as cosmetic additions to beautify the map. This means that the mod does not introduce additional game content alongside them, as is the case instead for those tied to Special buildings.

### For base game Special buildings

These are Special buildings that already exist in the base game, but that are given unique 3D models by the mod as they lack their own originally.

- Bamian Buddhas
- Great Sphinx of Giza (an addition to the Great Pyramids model)
- Nalanda University
- Kofuku Temple
- Prophetic Mosque

### Unique cities

These are 3D models of cities that played a uniquely important or interesting role historically. They are all hand-made and tailored to the historical layout and appearance of the cities, within the boundaries and limits of the in-game provinces to which they belong. These currently are:

- **Iberia** (Cordoba, Toledo)
- **France** (Paris, Carcassonne, Avignon)
- **HRE** (Cologne)
- **Italy** (Rome)
- **North Africa** (Mahdia, Alexandria, Cairo)
- **Eastern Europe** (Kyiv)
- **Middle East** (Baghdad)
- **Central Asia** (Ghazni)
- **India** (Delhi, Anuradhapura).
