# Traits — Congenital traits


  - *See also: Trait inheritance*

Congenital traits have a very small chance to appear upon birth and can be inherited by a character's descendants, especially if both parents have the same trait or are related. Congenital traits are marked with a white aura.

### Leveled congenital traits

The following Congenital traits come in multiple levels. A leveled Congenital trait has a 50% chance to increase in level if both parents have the same trait, both positively and negatively.

The game incorrectly states that if both parents have the same trait, it will get inherited (it will, just not as an active trait), the actual factors for genetic inheritance are complicated, see Family (relation). The name of some leveled traits depends on the character's gender.
