# Medieval Arts — Special buildings


The vast majority of Medieval Arts' content is introduced in the form of special buildings, all of which have an associated unique 3D model. Modifiers are generally kept lower compared to those from the game in order to avoid balancing issues, but overall are designed in a very similar way. If a special building does not start always constructed, then it can be built if its specific conditions are respected. Unlike buildings from the game, no individual Innovations are required, only Culture Eras. All buildings require 400 and 5 years to be completed.
