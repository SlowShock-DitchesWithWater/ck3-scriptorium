**The Way of Kings CK3** is a Total Conversion Mod for Crusader Kings III. It is set on the world of Roshar, the setting for The Stormlight Archive by fantasy author Brandon Sanderson. The mod is mostly known for its technical achievements, and many systematic features such as artifacts, literacy, marriage consent, lawsuits, dungeon delving, dueling, quests, fishing, radiant knighthood, and squireships. It also features several endgame victory conditions, including a hidden True Ending.

## Setting

The world of Roshar is the namesake of the Rosharan system, and its most striking aspect is the Highstorm, an enormous magically infused storm which ravages the planet at regular intervals. As it crosses the continents, the magical power of the storm is deposited into gemstones in the form of stormlight, which can then be utilized as a light source or to fuel magical abilities. The generic term for such fuel is investiture, and it is one of the core resources of the mod.

The shape of the Rosharan continent is a 2D slice of a 4D fractal called a

Julia set

. In fact, the Julia set in question is the one featured on Wikipedia.

As the Highstorms only blow one way, the mod models this with Exposed and Sheltered versions of vanilla terrain. For example, the eastern side of a mountain may consist of Exposed Mountains terrain, while the western side may consist of Sheltered Mountains. While Sheltered terrain tends to make for better places to construct holdings, they also limit the amount of investiture accessible by the Invested Warrior Men-at-Arms type.

### Geopolitics

Two Singers posing for a fashion magazine, illustrated by Dan dos Santos.

There are two playable species in WoK - humans and singers. The singers are partially crustacean humanoids who have marbled skin, four genders (male, female, malen and femalen), grow chitin from their body, and can change their forms during highstorms. As of the game's start date, most singers are enslaved by humans and are known as parshmen. The primary exception is the Listeners of the Shattered Plains, led by Queen Eshonai.

Fighting the Parshendi are the Alethi Highprinces, under King Elhokar Kholin of Alethkar. The Parshendi - literally "Parshmen who can think" in the Alethi language - assassinated Elhokar's father a few years ago, and he has called his vassals to the Shattered Plains to wage the War of Reckoning. Unfortunately, the Shattered Plains make for treacherous terrain, and the war has locked into a stalemate, as the Highprinces have grown more concerned with war profiteering than actually finishing the war.

West of Alethkar lies the Kingdom of Jah Keved, which is on the brink of collapse as the Highprinces prepare for a civil war upon the death of the current king. Nuatoma Mitthraw'nuruodo of Unkala sees his chance to ride out of the Horneater Peaks and claim a Shardblade of his own - which is a requirement for claiming the Kingdom of Unkala.

Further west, in the bureaucratic Azish Empire, several emperors have been assassinated by the same man over the past year, and the newest emperor has been elected as a sacrificial lamb - a young street urchin who nobody will miss. However, the Tukari high priest Tezim - secretly an immortal Herald - has declared himself God-King and has launched an invasion of Emul, a vassal state of Azir. He has refused to comply with court orders, and the bureaucracy is slow to react to the invasion, so for the time being Emul stands alone against the Herald.

### Technology

While many comparisons could be made with respect to various time periods in Earth's history, more is unique than is similar. All buildings must be constructed to withstand the highstorms, which only ever blow in one direction. Therefore, laits - areas that are naturally sheltered from the storm - are generally developed with higher levels of drainage technology than one might expect to be available.

Fabrial engineering is a growing field of study that concerns the mechanical applications of investiture. For example, Spanreeds allow people to communicate over great distances, whereas Painrials allow the manipulation of physical pain, which is useful in a surgical context. Soulcasters can transform matter - and the most useful of these can transform waste into food, mostly eliminating the need for supply lines.

Illness is comparatively rare, as the abundant investiture of Roshar has latent healing properties, and the Wisdom of the Heralds is a commonly known set of instructions for sanitation to maintain health among lowborn or highborn alike.

Due to the cycles of desolations that have ravaged Roshar for millennia, much ancient knowledge has been lost. Ancient fabrials were able to heal wounds, and powerful Shardblades could cut through anything except for its counterpart - the Shardplates. While not completely indestructible, Shardplate is incredibly resilient and grants a level of super strength to its wearer. Thus far, modern artifabrians have not been able to replicate these feats of ancient engineering, but recent advances have brought them closer to uncovering their secrets than ever before.

### Discrimination

Many faiths on Roshar practice some form of slavery, which ties in to the slavery system. Slaver faiths may be targeted by the Incite Slave Rebellion council task, while faiths with the Emancipation doctrine have access to special casus belli against slavers.

The near-mindless parshmen are reduced to pack animals for their human masters. The Listeners of Narak would like to see them freed from their bonds, but without a way to restore the parshmen's minds, it may not be feasible.

In many societies, Lighteyes dominate over Darkeyes, and a Darkeyed ruler would suffer general opinion penalties. Faiths that place importance on eyecolor also tend to consider red eyes to be a sign of possession by the void, and albino children are typically killed.

In the Vorin religion, a book on gendered traditions has been adopted as doctrine, which recognizes the feminine arts as the one-handed arts and the masculine arts as the two-handed arts. As such, reading and writing is considered taboo for men, whilst women are typically prevented from participating in warfare, or in any way using or even revealing their left-hands (called their "safehands") in public. Illiterate characters may take a decision to Learn to Read, which provides them with the Reader secret. An illiterate character who attempts to read Book Artifacts will have the contents rendered in the Women's Script font. Ardents - the equivalents of Monks and Nuns - are exempt from this rule, as they are not considered to have a gender.

### Governments and Electives

The Azish Empire has the Bureaucracy Government type, which has a reduced emphasis on warfare. Though it can certainly fight if needed, it prefers to resolve disputes via Lawsuits, an event-driven minigame that can be used to resolve claims. Domestically, a Bureaucracy has access to unprecedented customizability via Bureaucratic Policies and additional Vassal Contracts. A lot of these factors depend on the Bureaucratic Efficiency of a realm - which hinges on the County Opinion and Control levels among your capital and your vassals' capitals. This may be inspected in the Policy window of the Realms tab.

The Azish Elective is open to every citizen, and is primarily based on the writing of essays - the better your essay quality, the higher the viziers will think of you in the rankings when they elect the new Azish Prime. Your stats also have an impact, and a reputation for being a stellar administrator is certainly a boon. While the first application is free, submitting revisions costs money to discourage swamping the viziers with poor essay revisions.

The Eldric Kingdom of Babatharnam considers all authority to be derived from seniority. This is reflected by the default realm succession law being House Seniority, and by the Babath Elective, which elects the Most Ancient from across the eldest nobles of the realm. In theory, this is a very fair system, as everyone eventually gets their chance to rule if they live long enough. In practice, the Monavakah dynasty has held to power for the last fifty years, by murdering anyone old enough to pose a threat to their rule.

The Alethi and Veden have the Highprince Government type, which offers far more autonomy to the vassals than a regular Feudal system. The rank which would normally be considered an Emperor in a feudal system is only recognized as King in a Highprince government, as the Highprinces themselves effectively rule their own realms as kings. Highprinces have honed the art of war over centuries, and this is reflected by having increased War Tax income, being able to raid, and having access to a special Contest casus belli that encourage infighting among the lower ranks. However, during times of peace the Highprinces merely pay lip service to their King, providing reduces taxes and levies via vassal contracts.

The Luminocracy government type is the endgame government for those who are dedicated to playing as the Knights Radiant. It is unlocked by researching all of the Radiant Innovations, and heavily discourages relying on Dread and Tyranny. In exchange, you gain a lot of bonuses when stressed, and you unlock a secondary Radiant Council with nine Councillor slots available, providing new bonuses and tasks. You also have access to Bureaucratic Policies, and Luminocratic Dynasty heads gain access to the Radiance Dynasty Legacy track.

The Thaylen Elective is an elective among the merchants and naval officers, who pick the new monarch based on their proven ability to organize an economy. This typically weighs high stewardship, but also accrued wealth may be displayed in order to gain votes.

The Royal Army Tradition provides a Royal Guard for independent rulers. This, like the Radiant Council, is portrayed as a secondary council in addition to the regular one. The Royal Guard allows you to assign bodyguards to protect your dynasty members, or they may simply stay and protect you. The Royal Guard's Captain behaves essentially like a secondary marshal, while the size of your Royal Guard increases with your prestige level.

## Mechanics

Systematic mechanics are the bread and butter of Way of Kings gameplay, as it focuses more on emergent storytelling than on specific events.

### Artifacts

The Way of Kings CK3 features a rich Artifact system which has been commonly adopted by other Total Conversion Mods as well via the Way of Kings Artifacts and Dueling Module. The module is not required to access Artifacts in WoK.

There are four tiers of craftable artifacts which may be produced and acquired by anyone. There are also many unique artifacts, such as Shardblades, Shardplates and Soulcasters. Shardblades and Shardplates are indestructible, whereas a broken Soulcaster may be repaired by an Artifabrian, which is a new perk from the Artifabrian lifestyle tree.

### Investiture

Investiture is the generic term of magical power in the Cosmere, and is used to fuel magical devices and arts such as fabrials, healing, and surgebinding. It is also required in order to see in the darkness of the Dungeon Delve minigame, where running out can quickly spell doom.

Running out of investiture during normal gameplay usually only cancels any active surgebindings. However, certain effects such as wielding Nightblood or being possessed by Yelig-Nar will result in death if you run out of investiture during their effects.

Under normal conditions, a percentage of a ruler's income will be invested, and this ratio can be increased by claiming Perpendicularities. A ruler may also actively place their gemstones in the way of the Highstorm to rapidly charge their gems, but this comes with the risk of theft or the gems being blown away. Brave rulers or Knights Radiant who can safely traverse the storm may choose to stay outside in the storm to guard their gemstones.

### Knights Radiant

  - *Main article: Way of Kings/Knights Radiant*

The Knights Radiant are an organization of mentally ill glow-in-the-dark people who have weaponized therapy, and you can become one by having a stress break and bonding your very own therapy fairy, called a spren. There are ten orders of Knight Radiant and twelve spren types of relevance to gameplay, and your personality traits and actions shape your aptitude towards each order. Each order has five levels, which you progress through by swearing and upholding ideals that depend on your order's code of chivalry. Should your bonded spren decide that you are recreant, they will break the bond and you will find your house marred with a lowered ability to form bonds with spren in the future. However, becoming recreant in a way that kills your spren may result in getting a Shardblade.

#### Surgebinding

After swearing the second ideal, a Knight Radiant may spend investiture on surgebinding, which binds one of the Ten Surges - or fundamental forces of nature, as classified by in-world scholars - to the Knight's will. For example, Windrunners and Skybreakers share the Surge of Gravitation, which allows them to control gravity and effectively fly by falling through the air.

Each order can access two surges, each of which they share with another order. For example, the Windrunners bind the surges of Adhesion and Gravitation, whereas Skybreakers bind Gravitation and Division and Bondsmiths bind Tension and Adhesion.

Beyond providing special options in certain events, these all take the form of modifiers that drain a base rate of 10 investiture per month. As a character swears more ideals, they earn a discount on surgebinding, up to 50% discount at level 5.

The surges are, in order, Gravitation, Division, Abrasion, Progression, Illumination, Transformation, Transportation, Cohesion, Tension, and Adhesion.

#### Common thresholds

A Radiant of the First Ideal is able to heal from most wounds, illnesses and injuries by expending investiture.
 A Radiant of the Second Ideal unlocks the Surgebinding GUI, where they may spent investiture on various buffs.
 A Radiant of the Third Ideal may bond squires, and unlocks the Sprenblade, which is a stronger version of the Shardblade artifact. Bondsmiths are excluded from this.
 A Radiant of the Fourth Ideal unlocks the Sprenplate, which is a stronger version of the Shardplate artifact. Bondsmiths are excluded from this.
 A Radiant of the Fifth Ideal greatly improves in stats, rendering them effectively a demi-god of their particular field.

#### Squireships

Starting at third level, a Knight may choose Squires from among their friends and soulmates. One of the Radiance dynasty legacies expands this selection to also include vassals and wards, making it much easier to form squireships.

Successfully bonded Squires are granted the first level of the Knight's order, eliminating the randomization of the order. Squires still need to endure a stress break in order to bond a spren, as without a spren they are unable to advance beyond the First Ideal.

Cannibal Radiants are able to eat their dead squires.

#### Radiant Innovations and Luminocracy

A culture head who is Radiant has access to the Radiant Innovation associated to their order, starting in the second cultural era. These offer a varied set of bonuses, from stat buffs to men-at-arms-regiments and new decisions and interactions. After researching all ten Radiant Innovations, an eleventh Innovation is unlocked: Luminocracy, the Rule of Radiance. This provides a new government type, a special Radiant Council, and powerful realm Policies. Each Radiant Innovation also increases your chances of becoming a Radiant in the first place.

Luminocratic Dynasty heads also get access to a special Dynasty Legacy, the final of which unlocks the Restoration of the Oathpact decision, one of the Shardic Victories.

#### Tips and tricks

View the breakdown of your progression speed by mousing over the progression in the Radiant Window. All orders benefit from maintaining high Stress and are strongly penalized for Tyranny, but beyond that the progression speed is completely dependent on your order and bonded spren. You also benefit from actions which enact the ideals of your order, such as Skybreakers punishing criminals or Willshapers releasing prisoners.

Windrunners get a lot of extra squires.
 Skybreakers excel at Lawsuits.
 Edgedancers and Truthwatchers are able to heal other characters, not just themselves.
 All Knights Radiant of the third level of higher are able to teleport their commanded armies across the world in an instant, using Oathgates that are located in each Dawncity holding. Willshapers and Elsecallers, who are able to bind the Surge of Transportation, get a massive discount on the investiture cost to perform this action.
 Bondsmiths who own a Dawncity are able to instantly vassalize an AI-controlled Urithiru, which normally cannot be invaded.
 Squireships are a great option if you want to make sure that your heir stays the same order as you.

### Dungeon Delving

  - *Main article: Way of Kings/Dungeon Delve*

The ruler of Urithiru may go on a Dungeon Delve, which is an event-driven decision where your choices may affect the custom Dungeon Delve Combat minigame, which is played in the style of Darkest Dungeon. Running out of supplies or investiture during a delve is potentially fatal, so one is advised to stock up on significant amounts before starting a delve.

Many personality and lifestyle traits provide special abilities that can be utilized during Dungeon Delve combats, such as a Brave character's ability to Taunt, or an Arrogant character's ability to waste their turn Boasting, increasing their prestige but leaving them vulnerable to attacks. Each Dueling perk also provides a unique ability, and each of the ten orders of Knight Radiant also have a uniquely defined skill set, which changes as your character ranks up in their order. There are many other traits and artifacts that also provide abilities, but you are encouraged to find these during gameplay.

It is recommended to bring mostly Radiants and/or duelists, though Physicians can generally carry their weight even without any direct combat specializations.

### Dueling

There are several dueling systems in The Way of Kings CK3, and they all benefit from the new Dueling martial lifestyle tree. It retains the vanilla Single Combats system as one option. It also has a competitive dueling interaction, where one duels over some preset stake, such as artifacts, land, or prestige. Lastly there are Disadvantaged Duels, which utilize the Dungeon Delve combat system to fight against multiple opponents at once, but with potentially grander stakes and rewards.

### Fishing

In another custom minigame developed primarily by Castox, the player is able to go fish. If one manages to catch all 15 types of Fish, you get the Legendary Fisherman trait. Fishing further may grant Fish Hooks on other characters, and getting a second Fish Hook on the same character upgrades it to a Strong Fish Hook.

Fish Hooks otherwise behave exactly like normal Hooks.

### Shardic Victories

  - *Main article: Way of Kings/Shardic Victories*

There are many victory conditions to the Way of Kings CK3. These do not end the game, but provide huge long-term targets that you can work towards over hundreds of years. Each Shardic Victory provides gamechanging rewards.

By setting the Shardic Victories game rule to "All", you can see their requirements from the start of the game, otherwise you need to research the corresponding innovations.

There is a secret True Ending that can be discovered.

### Custom Pilgrimages

There are currently two custom pilgrimages - The Way of Kings pilgrimage to Urithiru, and the Nightwatcher Pilgrimage where you seek out the Nightwatcher and may be offered any Boon, at the cost of also getting a corresponding Curse. Choose your words carefully - there are always consequences to your request, some more drastic than others.

### Marriage and Consent

Many faiths on Roshar feature a courtship period before marriage, where any two characters must consent to marrying each other. Politics may dictate who courts who, but the characters decide whether it works out.

In the mod, this is modeled as the requirement that characters need the Friend, Best Friend, Lover, or Soulmate relations. A ruler can Arrange Courtship in much the same way as they would Arrange Marriage, but instead of marriage being instant this instead causes your candidate to launch a scheme of your choice against the target. These can include Sway, Befriend, Seduce or Romance.

Sway is generally a supporting scheme, as it does not usually result in relations. Seduce and Romance require sexual attraction, but are otherwise available to everyone. Romance tends to have lower success chance, whereas Seduce may result in secrets. Befriend can target anyone, but unless it's targeting Gregarious characters, it is limited to those who have the Befriend perk or the last Adventure Dynasty Legacy perk.

Arranged courtships for Children usually take the form of Wardships, where your candidate is encouraged to become friends with the corresponding candidate.

#### Destined Canon Spouses

Note: This section may contain spoilers for the books as well as stuff that's made up for the mod.

Because they are characters that many people in the fandom are invested in the shipping of, several characters have been declared as mod-canon spouses, which means that the AI will not pair them up with other characters unless the player intervenes. At a random point in time over the first decade, they will get married unless the player has intervened with them in some way. The list is:

- Shallan Davar and Adolin Kholin
- Hoid Maxtori and Jasnah Kholin
- Dalinar Kholin and Navani Kholin
- Kaladin Stormblessed and Depression
