# Artifacts — Creating artifacts


For rulers the main way of obtaining artifacts is via the Commission Artifact decision and for adventurers the main way of obtaining artifacts is by visiting City holdings and buying them with  Gold. Rulers can also use the Commission Epic decision, which will create a Book artifact if the character has a royal court and a Journal artifact if the character does not have a royal court.

If a character wins a game of chess there is a 5% chance to gain a trinket artifact called Lucky Go Board if the character is in Eastern Asia and *Lucky Chess Board* if the character is somewhere else. The chance is increased to 25% if the game was played for a county. When offered, the artifact can also be destroyed to lose 10  Stress or improved for a moderate amount of  Gold.

If the  Tours and Tournaments DLC is installed, winning  Grand Tournament contests will reward artifacts. Their rarity depends on the Prizes activity option. All contests can award trinket or pedestal item artifacts, but certain contests can also award weapon, armor or bow artifacts.

If the  All Under Heaven DLC is installed, tributaries of China can obtain a Fine Seal of Investiture regalia by paying tribute while they have sufficient Imperial Grace.

If the  Roads to Power DLC is installed a Golden Coat of the Swan armor can be obtained via the Knight of the Swan decision.

If the  By God Alone DLC is installed pedestal items can also be created by extracting a relic from a canonized character.

If a character has a Royal Court artifacts can be obtained the following ways:

- When a pet dies there will be the option to pay 10-20 Gold to gain a small wall ornament.
- Book artifacts can be obtained via the University Visit activity.
- If a character wins a battle against a Noble opponent there is a chance to gain a Warbanner large wall ornament. Alternatively the artifact can be destroyed to gain 75 Prestige.
- If a character has the Arbitrary, Deceitful, Callous, Sadistic or Vengeful trait executing a prisoner has a 25% chance to be offered a Skull pedestal item. If the prisoner was a Nemesis the option will always be given. If the ruler does not have an Executioner it will lose **-30** Opinion with the Personal Physician or Antiquarian.
- If the Royal Court DLC is installed, all characters will have a House Banner large wall ornament. If their last Kingdom or above title the character who took the last title will get the artifact but if they're not part of the same house only the Court Grandeur bonus will be given.
- If the All Under Heaven DLC is installed, all independent rulers with Celestial, Meritocratic and Meritocratic Khanate government as well as the owner of the Chrysanthemum Throne title will have an Imperial Seal pedestal item.
- If the All Under Heaven DLC is installed, tributaries of rulers with Mandala government unlock the Create Bunga Mas decision, which can create a pedestal item.
- If the Friends and Foes DLC is installed, when a nemesis dies characters with the Callous, Sadistic or Vengeful trait can gain a *Skull Goblet* pedestal item. The option will not be available if the nemesis' rite has the Sky Burials funerary tradition.

### Hunt trophies

Successful  Hunt activities will grant trinket artifacts. If the character has a Royal Court and the activity variant was not  Falconry, the artifacts can also be wall ornaments. These artifacts will always give monthly  Prestige and depending on their rarity will also have additional effects.

- Foxes, gazelles, hares, roes and non-exotic birds will always be **Common**
- Hyenas, lynxes, wolves and exotic birds have a 97% chance to be **Common** and a 3% chance to be **Masterwork**
- Antelopes, aurochs, bisons, reindeers and stags have a 50% chance to be **Common** and a 50% chance to be **Masterwork**
- Bears, leopards, lions and tigers have an 85% chance to be **Masterwork** and a 15% chance to be **Famed**
- Boars, elks and harts have a 33% chance to be **Masterwork** and a 67% chance to be **Famed**
- If the activity variant was Legendary then the rarity will be doubled
- A few rare events during the activity can also increase the rarity

### Inspirations

|  | Available only with the Royal Court DLC *or* the Roads to Power DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Landless characters occasionally gain an Inspiration, allowing them to work on creating an artifact. Characters older than 13 can sponsor the inspirations with  Gold, with the cost depending on how skilled the inspired character is. Once an inspired character is financed they will start working on the artifact. The artifact's effects will depend on the inspired character's traits. The artifact rarity is decided by an average of the relevant skills plus modifiers from some cultures, buildings or events. There are three ways for characters to gain inspirations:

- Inspired characters can randomly appear in royal courts. The greater a court's Grandeur the higher the chance that an inspired character will appear.
- Followers of adventurers who have the Inspiration is Everywhere lifestyle perk can randomly gain inspirations.
- Entourage members may gain a Book inspiration if liege adopted Scholasticism as a personal tenet.

There are multiple types of Inspiration, which determine what type of artifact will be created. If an inspiration can create more than one type of artifact there's a small chance the sponsor will be allowed to choose what sort of artifact they want created.

### Travels journal

|  | Available only with the Roads to Power DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Adventurers can take the *The Travels of [adventurer name]* decision to create a Journal artifact with the same name. Based on the number of points of interest visited, the artifact will give the following:

| Points of Interest visited | Lifestyle Experience | Rarity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **26-35** | **+10%** | **Common** |
| **36-47** | **+20%** | **Masterwork** |
| **48-59** | **+30%** | **Famed** |
| **60+** | **+40%** | **Illustrious** |

### Icons

|  | Available only with the Roads to Power DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Icons are artifacts that can be created via the *Commission Icon* decision. There are three artifact options to choose from when taking the decision:

All characters can choose to create a Trinket artifact. For landed rulers the artifact will have **Masterwork** rarity and provide **+0.3** or **+0.4** monthly  Piety. For landless rulers there is a small chance the artifact will have **Common** rarity and provide **+0.1** or **+0.2** monthly  Piety.

Rulers with a royal court an choose to create a Large Wall Ornament or Small Wall Ornament artifact. The artifact will have two of the following bonuses:

- Monthly piety
- Temple holding and building construction cost reduction
- Zealot vassal tax contribution
- Zealot vassal opinion
- Clergy opinion
