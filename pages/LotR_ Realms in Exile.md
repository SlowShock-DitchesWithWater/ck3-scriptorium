## !!FAQ!!

### This FAQ was taken from the Discord.

---

**Is there a roadmap, what is the general plan for future updates?**

Yes, we do have a roadmap! We will not give hard dates because that’s just setting up for disappointment, but here is the rough outline for upcoming updates.

- **Realms 4.3** - The lands of Mûmakan! The home of countless tribes and the mighty Mûmakil, long has the shadow grown and reigned over Mûmakan. Now as Sauron’s power waxes ever greater, it stands poised to snuff out what resistance remains in the region…
- **Realms 5.0** - At long last, a fellowship will be gathered, and the great battle of our time shall be decided. Join the Fellowship of the Ring on their question to destroy the One, as all of Middle-earth rumbles towards the final confrontation of the age.
- **Realms 6.0** - “The road goes ever on and on…”

---

**What CK3 patch should the present release be played on?**

The current release is compatible with the latest CK3 release, patch 1.17.1 (Ascendant).

---

**What races are playable in the mod?**

Currently, you are able to play as most of the races of Middle-earth: Humans, Elves, Dwarves, Hobbits, and Orcs (and some Maiar). At this time there are no serious plans to add any further, more esoteric races (such as Ents, Great Eagles, or Giant Spiders) as playable on account of the amount of work (both 3d asset and coding) required to implement them in an immersive way.

---

**Is the Fellowship and their Quest in the mod?**

Not yet, to make it short. We look forward to giving the epic tale of the Fellowship a proper telling once all the major regions and races are implemented.

---

**Where is the One Ring?**

At our start date, in Bilbo’s pocket, most likely! But in reply to your actual question, at this time Sauron cannot retrieve the One Ring, and likely will not be able to until the Ring-quest is added in 5.0. The One Ring will be an artifact once added.

---

**Will there be more start dates?**

Currently we have two implemented start dates - T.A. 3000, the War of the Ring, and T.A. 2754, the Long Winter. We are currently working on developing T.A. 1349, the Rise of Angmar. Other planned start dates can be found in the timeline bar at the top of the bookmark screens. Please stay tuned for more!

---

**Will you do the First Age and Second Age?**

At this point in time, no. Our earliest possible start date will be post-Akallabeth, Second Age 3320, with the founding of Arnor and Gondor. CK3’s map cannot be dynamically changed model the sinking of Beleriand and Númenor. One day perhaps we will do a submod for those eras, but for the foreseeable future, no.

---

**What are your sources and inspirations?**

At heart, this is the team’s interpretation of Tolkien’s legendarium. But we generally try to remain true to the canon text (priority given to the Lord of the Rings, Hobbit, and Silmarillion), at least one version of it in cases where they contradict. Sometimes we will make pragmatic adaptations for the sake of playability or interesting dynamics.

Visually we lean heavily on Peter Jackson’s films, both the Hobbit and the Lord of the Rings trilogies.

At this point we have no plans to adapt, adopt or integrate any elements from Amazon's "Rings of Power" series.

Where Tolkien’s text falls silent, we take inspiration from officially licensed third-party sources and our sister-mod The Age of the Ring. Our expanded canon is known as Realms Lore. We bring our own vision to the world of Middle-earth.

---

**How Does Númenórean Blood Work?**

Númenórean blood works through a mix of morality and blood inheritance. Unlike regular genetic traits, it cannot be ‘upgraded’ through mixing two low tier traits. Sinning increases the chance of your blood degrading to a lower tier with the next generation (potentially losing it completely), whereas being virtuous when you have a marriage between two different tiers increases the chance of the higher tier inheriting.

---

**Can Elves and Humans marry? What happens to their children if so?**

Due to the incredibly rare nature of intermarriage between Elves and Men in the canon legendarium (under 5 in 7000 years), the ability for the two to intermarry or romance will be restricted to specific pre-scripted events only (IE, Arwen and Aragorn). Children of such unions will be considered for all intents and purpose Men – the Choice of Kindred will only apply to the children of Elrond.

---

**What is your DLC policy?**

We will, generally, try to ensure the mod remains playable to those both with and without DLCs. However, there may come a point where a DLC adds a system we view as so fundamental and critical to the mod we will require it. Suffice to say, it will be on a per-DLC basis, and the mod description will reflect that.

---

**Can I Donate?**

We truly appreciate all the support the community gives, but we do this because it is our passion and out of love for both CK3 and Tolkien’s universe, not for monetary compensation.

If you wish to contribute artistic works or commission third-party artists to produce assets for the mod, please contact a Lead Developer beforehand. This ensures we are aligned on the project requirements and desired end results before proceeding.

---

**Can I acquire or upgrade the Sorcerer trait ?**

No, for the moment there is no way to learn sorcery (Rhûn campaign excepted), or to upgrade the "Sorcerer" traits. We are working on mechanics that will come in a future update to allow it.

---

**When does my character reach adulthood ?**

- **Humans and Orcs** - 16
- **Hobbits** - 33
- **Dwarves** - 40
- **Elves** - 50

---

**When can my character get married ?**

- **Humans and Orcs** - 16
- **Hobbits** - 33
- **Dwarves** - 70
- **Elves** - 50
- **Blood of Numenór** - check the trait tooltip

---

**I want to go on a mining expedition activity, how do I do that ?**

There are two ways to enable mining expeditions :

- be a Dwarf
- Make sure your culture has the "Miners" cultural tradition. (Anyone can adopt it, Noldor start with it already)

You'll then get access to expeditions through your Activities tab

---

**Should I download the beta version or the main mod?**

We are not currently in an open beta phase. Please do not use the beta version of the mod, it is outdated.

## About

***LotR: Realms in Exile*** is a total conversion mod for Crusader Kings 3 set in Middle-Earth, bringing to life the diverse characters in J. R. R. Tolkien’s The Lord of the Rings. This is an unofficial wiki for the mod's Gameplay Mechanics. A wiki containing ***Realm's Lore*** the expanded canon included in the mod can be found here.
