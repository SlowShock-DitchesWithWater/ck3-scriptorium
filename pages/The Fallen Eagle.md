# The Fallen Eagle

> [!] Game modification
>
> [!] : The Fallen Eagle

The Fallen Eagle is a total conversion mod that brings the Early Medieval Era (361 – 867) to Crusader Kings 3. With only 361, 395, 476, and 532 currently available as bookmarks, the mod will eventually include other starting dates in 632 and 768. By the time the mod will be complete with all planned starting dates, one can govern the fragile Western Roman Empire, fill the void in the absence of Roman rule in Western Europe, wage wars of reconquest under the Eastern Roman banner, march forth beyond the deserts of Arabia, or establish a domain rightfully seen as a successor to Rome. In this age of changing tides and constant struggle, perhaps one can shape the tumultuous lands of Europe, Africa, and Asia as they see fit.

**Features**

- Playable Bookmarks for the years 361, 395, 476, and 532 AD
- A detailed political and cultural layout of Europe, Africa, and Asia during the Early Medieval Era
- Unique and playable historical figures, including Flavius Stilicho, Alaric, Odoacer, Belisarius, and many others
- A unique interface and GUI
- A complete soundtrack composed by George Hammond
- Immersive cultures, religions, governments, and mechanics
- Detailed 3D Assets (clothing, armor, and headgear) made by ツALU, Atreides, and Celticus
