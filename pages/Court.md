# Court

> [!] This article has been verified for the current PC version (1.20) of the game.

Court positions for a ruler

Each noble character has a court, which consists of the unlanded characters at a character's court. A court may contain family members, knights, lovers, and courtiers or followers. Rulers may also have hostages and guests. Your Prisoners are found in a tab under the "Court positions"-page.
