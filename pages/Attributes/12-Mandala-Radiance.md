# Attributes — Mandala Radiance


|  | Available only with the All Under Heaven DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

  - *Main article: Mandala*

Every ruler with  Mandala government has a **Mandala Radiance** value (between 0 and 100), which depends on their Level of Devotion, level of Mandala Aspect, perks, whether they are independent or not, and number of Capital Temple Complex buildings and decreases with distance from the capital. Mandala Radiance affects the acceptance of other rulers to become tributaries. If the Capital Temple Complex building in the realm capital is ruined, Mandala Radiance will be set to 0.
