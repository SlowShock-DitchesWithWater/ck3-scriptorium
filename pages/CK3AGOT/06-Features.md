# CK3AGOT — Features


The mod adds or reskins many mechanics in order to create a more immersive A Song of Ice and Fire feel.

### Mega Wars

Experience immersive, realm-wide wars like never before. The **Mega Wars** system expands opportunities for lesser vassals to take what is rightfully theirs. Players whose lieges go to war can take sides or remain neutral. Rebels who fail in their aspirations can face punishments ranging from losing their titles or watching their dynasty vanish before their very eyes. Chaos is a ladder, its rungs wrought by the complicated system of alliances and sympathies to lords high and low.

*“IT IS SAID with truth that every building is constructed stone by stone, and the same may be said of knowledge, extracted and compiled by many learned men, each of whom builds upon the works of those who preceded him. What one of them does not know is known to another, and little remains truly unknown if one seeks far enough.” ― George R.R. Martin, quote from The World of Ice and Fire: The Untold History of Westeros and the Game of Thrones*

### Kingsguard

  - *Main article: CK3AGOT/Kingsguard* As the ruler of the Seven Kingdoms, your safety is paramount. The **Kingsguard** known poetically as the White Swords or White Cloaks, are the royal bodyguards of the King of the **Iron Throne**. Regarded as the finest knights in all the **Seven Kingdoms**, they are sworn to protect their king and the royal family with their very lives, to obey his commands, and to keep his secrets. The Kingsguard are sworn for life and are forbidden from owning land, taking a wife, or fathering children, although they can hold non-hereditary titles, such as being **warden** or The **Hand of the King**.
- "*Just once in my life, before it's over, I want to know what it's like to serve with pride, to fight for someone I believe in***."** — *Ser Barristan (The Bold) Selmy*

### Expanded Councilors

  - *Main article: CK3AGOT/Small Council*

The **Hand of the King**, or the Castellan for lesser lords, is one of the most powerful councilors at your disposal. Assigning them to a variety of tasks based on their aptitude across all lifestyles. When Ruling the Iron Throne, if your **Lords Paramount** are not competent enough for the complexities of the **Small Council**, you may call upon their vassals to serve the realm dutifully instead.

- " *Should I be worried? Janos Slynt, Pycelle...the Small Council grows smaller every day*. " — *Varys*

### Knights and Squires

As a ruler, you have the option to offer budding young men, and exceptional women, a chance to squire for you with the chances of becoming a knight. Along the way, you'll experience plenty of opportunities to train your squire and track their progress.

### The Night’s Watch & Beyond The Wall

  - *Main article: CK3AGOT/Struggle Beyond the Wall*

Take the Black as the Lord Commander of **The** **Night’s Watch**, with the goal of antagonizing the wildling kingdoms far beyond the shadow of The Wall. As a Wildling, participate in the Struggle Beyond The Wall, uniting the fractious free folk by sword or song.

- *Night gathers, and now my watch begins. It shall not end until my death. I shall take no wife, hold no lands, father no children. I shall wear no crowns and win no glory. I shall live and die at my post. I am the sword in the darkness. I am the watcher on the walls*. — *The Men of The Nights Watch*

### Deeper Roleplay

  - *Main article: CK3AGOT/Dynasty*

  - *Main article: CK3AGOT/Individuals*

For custom characters, there are new opportunities to blend seamlessly into the world of Ice and Fire. Use the House Customizer to join an existing House or revive a long-dead dynasty, or forge your backstory with the help your maester, who will preserve your glorious history for generations to come.

- " *When you play a game of thrones, you win or you die." - Unknown*

### Armors, Crowns, Artifacts, and Swords

  - *Main article: CK3AGOT/Artifacts*

Included are armors for each of the Seven Kingdoms, as well as many new ancestral valyrian steel swords and so many crowns and artifacts for every conceivable kingdom.

- A mind needs books as a sword needs a whetstone, if it is to keep its edge." — Tyrion Lannister

### More Cultures & Religions

  - *Main article: CK3AGOT/Cultures and Traditions*

  - *Main article: CK3AGOT/Religions and Faiths*

8,000 years of history have resulted in 40 distinct, playable cultures in Westeros, each with unique traditions and Men-at-Arms suited to their homelands. Each religion, from the Faith of the Seven to the Cold Gods, brings new unique doctrines and holy sites for you to worship as your desire.

*"We ask the Father to judge us with mercy, accepting our human frailty. We ask the Mother to bless our crops so we may feed ourselves and all who come to our door. We ask the Warrior to give us courage in these days of strife and turmoil. We ask the Maiden to protect Sally's virtue to keep her from the clutches of depravity" —* A pious Riverlands farmer praying to the Seven

### Spy Networks

  - *Main article: CK3AGOT/Lifestyles*

Build a network of little birds throughout the realm, spying on rival lords, collecting their secrets at a pace worthy of the excessive subterfuge to climb the ladder in Westeros.

- *" Chaos isn't a pit, Chaos is a ladder."* — Petyr Baelish

### An Enormous Map

  - *Main article: CK3AGOT/Titles and Holdings*

Experience Westeros in a size never before seen. The continent contains over 6,100 baronies+, with over 1,000 playable counties. In future releases, this will expand to include - Essos and The Free Cities, Valyria and The Summer Sea and Islands, The Jade Sea, The Great Dothraki Sea, and beyond.

### A Meticulously Detailed Map

  - *Main article: CK3AGOT/Buildings*

From the Frozen Shore beyond The Wall to the Red Mountains of Dorne, our map portrays Westeros as if it were an extension of the real world. Each kingdoms’ capital has its own custom model, as well as most of the major cities on the continent and some minor castles. Our focus on canonical and ecological accuracy will fulfil all of your map-staring needs.

### Roads, Roads, Roads

Guide your doomstacks along roads that are more than eye-candy- with two different tiers imparting higher supply limits and movement speeds to facilitate the most dangerous of doomstacks.
