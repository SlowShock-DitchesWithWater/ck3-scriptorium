# Tenet — Tenet popularity


Christianity tracks a value for each tenet called Tenet Popularity, which affects whether AI characters will support it or oppose it during  Ecumenical Council activities as well as the chance that it will be targeted by a Papal Bull decision. If a rite is the Mainline Rite it can only be modified with tenets that have at least 800 Tenet Popularity. If Tenet Popularity reaches 2000 the head of rite will always declare it Permitted. Tenet Popularity is calculated by the following:

- **+1** per county that follows a rite that has the tenet as core tenet
- **+1** per Baron that has the tenet as personal tenet
- **+2** per Count that has the tenet as personal tenet
- **+3** per Duke that has the tenet as personal tenet
- **+4** per King that has the tenet as personal tenet
- **+5** per Emperor that has the tenet as personal tenet
- **+6** per Hegemon that has the tenet as personal tenet
- **+10** per Monastic Order that has the tenet
- **+40** per Evangelize Personal Tenet decision without the third Servants of God legacy
- **+60** per Evangelize Personal Tenet decision with the third Servants of God legacy
- **+40** if the new head of faith has the tenet as a personal tenet
- **+40** if the tenet becomes Permitted
- **-100** if the tenet becomes Prohibited
- **-100** if tenet is a core tenet and its rite is declared heresy
- Tenet popularity can also be changed by the outcome of Ecumenical Council activities
