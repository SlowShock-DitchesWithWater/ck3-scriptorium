# Doctrines — Marriage doctrines


Marriage doctrines determine who a character can marry, when can they marry or divorce, and the status of children born out of formal relationships.

| Category | Doctrine | Effects | Divergence | Max piety cost | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marriage Type | Monogamous | Characters can have one spouse Converting to the faith removes secondary spouses unless culture tradition allows it **-40** Opinion if spouse has secondary spouses and can divorce them **-15** Opinion if spouse has a concubines and can divorce them An event when first marrying gives a choice between **+350** prestige or a scaled amount of gold | 30% if Polygamous 20% if Consorts & Concubines | 200 if Monogamous 400 if Polygamous 400 if Consorts & Concubines | *Marriage is a sacred bond between two individuals. Attempting to form such a bond with multiple people at once is sacrilege.* |
| Marriage Type | Polygamous | Characters can have one primary spouse and 3 secondary spouses **-15** Opinion if spouse has a concubines and can divorce them Having fewer spouses than required by the primary title rank gives **-0.50** Piety per missing spouse | 30% if Monogamous 10% if Consorts & Concubines | 400 if Monogamous 200 if Polygamous 400 if Consorts & Concubines | *Since marriage is a holy union, having multiple marriages is clearly more holy than having just a single marriage — so long as each marriage is holy in and of itself.* |
| Marriage Type | Consorts & Concubines | Characters can have one spouse Rulers can have up to three (male) consorts or (female) concubines **-40** Opinion if spouse has secondary spouses and can divorce them Having fewer consorts/concubines than required by the primary title rank gives **-0.10** Prestige per missing one | 20% if Monogamous 10% if Polygamous | 400 if Monogamous 400 if Polygamous 200 if Consorts & Concubines | *Marriage is a sacred bond, but as long as their spouse takes priority one may be permitted to have concubines or consorts to engage with as well.* |
| Divorce | Disallowed | Characters cannot divorce | 5% if Must be Approved 15% if Always Allowed | 100 if Disallowed 200 if Must be Approved 300 if Always Allowed | *The vows of marriage are final and binding. Only death may do us part.* |
| Divorce | Must be Approved | If there is a spiritual head of faith, secular characters require its approval to divorce If there is no spiritual head of faith, secular characters require the approval of the House Head or Dynasty Head to divorce Clergy characters require the approval of the House Head or Dynasty Head to divorce | 5% if Disallowed 10% if Always Allowed | 200 if Disallowed 100 if Must be Approved 200 if Always Allowed | *While the bonds of marriage should not be taken lightly, extenuating circumstances can sometimes make divorce a necessity.* |
| Divorce | Always Allowed | Characters can divorce at the cost of 100 Piety | 15% if Disallowed 10% if Must be Approved | 300 if Disallowed 200 if Must be Approved 100 if Always Allowed | *Marriage is merely a partnership between two people, and like all partnerships, can be ended at will.* |
| Bastardry | No Bastards | Children born outside marriage or concubinage have the Wild Oat trait | 5% if Legitimization 10% if No Legitimization | 200 if No Bastards 400 if Legitimization 600 if No Legitimization | *Blood determines lineage, not marriage. Children born out of wedlock are just as legitimate as any other.* |
| Bastardry | Legitimization | Children born outside marriage or concubinage have the Bastard trait House Heads and Dynasty Heads can replace the trait with Legitimized Bastard | 5% if No Bastards 5% if No Legitimization | 400 if No Bastards 200 if Legitimization 400 if No Legitimization | *Marriage is holy, so children born outside of it must be blessed in another way if they are to be considered legitimate.* |
