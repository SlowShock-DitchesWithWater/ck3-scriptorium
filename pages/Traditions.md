# Traditions

> [!] Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
> [!] At least some were last verified for version 1.18.
>
> [!] This article is for the PC version of Crusader Kings 3 only.

| “ | Traditions represent the practices, habits, values, and ethic of individual Cultures. Each Tradition grants some type of bonus or special ability to Characters or Counties of that Culture.The Culture Head can establish new Traditions or replace existing ones for a Culture through Reformation. Cultures can have up to 6 Traditions, but most have fewer. — In-game encyclopedia | ” |
| --- | --- | --- |

Cultural Traditions are interchangable bonuses which provide unique effects to cultures. Every culture starts with at least two, but there is no minimimum and a hybridized culture can have zero. Despite what the in-game encyclopedia says there is a maximum of 8 traditions to a culture.

- Base: **5**
- Early Medieval: **+1**
- High Medieval: **+1**
- Late Medieval: **+1**
