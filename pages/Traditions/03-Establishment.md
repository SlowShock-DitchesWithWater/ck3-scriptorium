# Traditions — Establishment


|  | Available only with the Royal Court DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Establishing a new tradition takes time, during the process none of the effects apply. If the tradition is replacing another the effects of the old tradition are removed immediately. The time it will take to establish depends largely on the number of counties the culture is present in:

- If the tradition is present in ≤5 counties establishment will take 5 years **(no penalty)**.
- If the tradition is present in 6-29 counties establishment will take the same amount of time as the # of counties. Example: 16 counties = 16 years **(scaling penalty)**.
- If the tradition is present in ≥30 counties establishment will take 30 years **(flat penalty)**.
- If the tradition is replacing another it will add **+25%**.

### Cooldown

After a new tradition have been established there is a  **50** year cooldown period where traditions cannot be modified.

This is affected by the  Explore Cultures discount (Kurultai Member)
