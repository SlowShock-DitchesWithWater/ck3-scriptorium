# Attributes — Dread


Dread is a measure of how feared a ruler is by their vassals and courtiers. A character's dread can range from 0 to 100, with  Nomadic and  Meritocratic Khanate rulers having a higher cap of 150. Enough dread compared to another character's boldness can make characters either intimidated or terrified, allowing most diplomatic actions to succeed despite negative opinion.

Dread is gained by various actions that inspire fear, such as torturing or executing prisoners. However, it is also possible to lose dread through actions showing compassion or weakness, including releasing prisoners unconditionally and accepting faction demands. Each character additionally has *natural dread* which is the value towards which dread will drift over time (either up or down), at a base rate of 0.5 per month. The lifestyle perk *Forever Infamous* prevents dread from decaying.

Each character has a hidden boldness attribute, which is decided primarily by its traits (i.e characters with "Brave" trait will never be Intimidated, while those with "Craven" trait will always be Terrified). A character becomes intimidated by characters whose  dread is 20 above their own boldness and terrified by characters whose dread is 45 above their own boldness. This includes negative boldness, meaning that characters with 0  dread can still have 'Intimidated' or 'Terrified' characters if they are below the boldness level required.

### Intimidation and terror

Intimidated characters are less likely to join factions or schemes against the character they're intimidated by, while a terrified character will never join factions or schemes against them. In addition, it affects their acceptance for various character interactions:

| Interaction | Intimidated acceptance | Terrified acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrange marriage | **+75** | **+150** |
| Blackmail | **+50** | **+100** |
| Negotiate alliance | **+50** | **+100** |
| Offer guardianship | **+50** | **+100** |
| Offer ward | **+50** | **+100** |
| Ransom | **+40** | **+100** |
| Ask to take vows | **+25** | **+50** |
| Imprison | **+25** | **+50** |
| Retract vassal | **+25** | **+50** |
| Revoke title | **+25** | **+50** |
| Demand conversion | **+10** | **+20** |
| Offer vassalage | **+10** | **+20** |
| Marry | **-50** | **-75** |
