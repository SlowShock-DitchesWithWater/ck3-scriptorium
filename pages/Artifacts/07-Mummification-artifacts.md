# Artifacts — Mummification artifacts


|  | Available only with the Legends of the Dead DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

If a rite has the Mummification doctrine, artifacts can be obtained at the end of  Funeral activities. Every ruler can choose to gain a Trinket artifact, while those who have a Royal Court can choose to gain Furniture instead. These artifacts require the faith to have the doctrine and give **+0.2** monthly  Prestige otherwise.

The rarity of the artifact depends on the traits (especially if they are virtues or sins) and title rank of the deceased as well as whether they were a head of faith. Every ruler can choose the artifact to be a Trinket and rulers who have a Royal Court can choose the artifact to be Furniture instead.

| Rarity | Mummy effects | Heart effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Common** | **+0.2** Monthly Piety **+1** Court Grandeur | **+0.1** Monthly Piety **+0.1%** Prestige Gain per Dread |
| **Masterwork** | **+0.4-0.8** Monthly Piety **+2-4** Court Grandeur **+5** Zealot Vassal Opinion (33% chance) | **+0.2-0.6** Monthly Piety **+0.1%** Prestige Gain per Dread **+4** Court Grandeur (33% chance) **+5** Zealot Vassal Opinion (33% chance) |
| **Famed** | **+1** Monthly Piety **+5** Court Grandeur **+10** Zealot Vassal Opinion | **+0.75** Monthly Piety **+0.2%** Prestige Gain per Dread **+10** Zealot Vassal Opinion |
| **Illustrious** | **+2** Monthly Piety **+10** Court Grandeur **+15** Zealot Vassal Opinion **+5%** Legitimacy Gain **+1** Learning per Level of Devotion | **+1** Monthly Piety **+0.2%** Prestige Gain per Dread **+15** Zealot Vassal Opinion **+2%** Legitimacy Gain |
