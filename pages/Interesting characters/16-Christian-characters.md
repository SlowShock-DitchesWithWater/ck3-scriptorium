# Interesting characters — Christian characters


### Catholic

#### Jerusalem

- **Baldwin IV "the Leper" Anjou, King of Jerusalem**: When he was born, his godfather and uncle, King Baldwin III, joked that his gift to the child would be the Kingdom of Jerusalem. Through unforeseen circumstances, the boy would be named the heir to the kingdom in 1174 when the king died of dysentery. Suffering from a handicap from his early days that eventually developed into leprosy, Baldwin IV ascended to the throne of Jerusalem at only the age of 13, to rule under a regent for 2 years. 2 years ago, you took the throne of Jerusalem for yourself. Historically, in 7 years, you will be dead. Saladin, the legendary founder of the Ayyubid dynasty sits across the Sinai, eyeing your lands with envy and ambition. Within a century, the Ayyubids will destroy the kingdom of Jerusalem. Can you change fate?
- **Sibylla d'Anjou, Countess of Ghazza:** Jerusalem has already known one queen in its brief history and it seems ti will soon see another. Sibylla's brother, KIng Baudouin, has ruled ably despite his leprosy, but it is only a matter of time before the malady claims his life. A young widow and her brother's acknowledged heir, Sibylla's hand is eagerly sought by many, and a strong alliance would go a long way toward strengthening her position. Jerusalem's position is perilous and its king not long for this world. Can yo ascend the throne and ensure it not only survives, but thrives?
- **Raimon de Toulouse, Duke of Lebanon:** Robbed of his father at an early age by Nizari assassins, Raimon has fought against Turkmen raiders since he was seventeen, hired corsairs to scourge Byzantine shores, and endured nearly a decade of captiveity in a Zengid prison. Upon his release, he was a natural choice a regent for the child-king Baudouin, but that leprous young man is now of age and Raimon has returned to his lands to contemplate his kingdom's future, as well as his own. You have tasted and relinquished the power of a king, and you remain a pillar of the realm. What will you do with your influence?

#### England

- **Henry II Plantagenet, King of England**: Henry established himself in both England and the continent but at a high personal cost. He is estranged from his wife and 2 sons and historically died 2 years after his sons rebelled. Although Richard is the Lionheart, let the old lion roar one last time in his twilight, and put his house in order before he departs.

#### France

- **Richard Plantagenet**, **Count of Poitiers**: Prince Richard, your brother, the heir of England is ailing. His death would put you first in succession for the Kingdom of England. Historically, Prince Richard would go on to become Richard the Lionheart, joining the Third Crusade against Saladin and leading the Crusade at times. However, your path is your own and you have the military prowess to back your words with fire and steel no matter where you go. Your destiny may lie somewhere outside of France, leaving your land as an adventurer and refusing the call to England, or perhaps staying and fighting for a Norman realm in France. You may also stay in France, adopting to the culture that you've come to live around and show the rebels you put down 4 years ago what a real French revolution looks like, throwing your father's kingdom out of the land and claiming the French throne for yourself.

#### Holy Roman Empire

- **Friedrich "Barbarossa**" **Hohenstaufen, Kaiser of the Holy Roman Empire**: One of the greatest rulers of Germany in the medieval era, Friedrich Barbarossa is a veteran of the second crusade and a series of campaigns in Italy which ended with a sharp decline in feudalism in northern Italy. Your glorious campaigns are behind you for the most part, yet the Third Crusade is not far off. Historically you met your end while traveling to the holy land, drowning while attempting to ford a river. Despite your age, your legend is not yet over, and the story of your lineage is just beginning.
- **Friedrich III Hohenzollern, Count of Zollern**: A meager count in 1178 with a singular province, your family lineage will historically forge Germany into a modern empire. Historically, your loyalty to the Habsburg and Hohenstaufen families will see you rewarded many holdings, yet your path to greatness does not need to be servitude. Can the age of a Hohenzollern Kaiser be brought about nearly a millennia sooner?

#### Iberia

- **Afonso "the Conqueror" Borgonha, King of Portugal**: An old king of a young kingdom; you are the first king of Portugal, having won the right through warfare and revolution. Having fought off both Galicians and Moors many decade ago, your kingdom has expanded and held it's ground, carving your mark into Iberia over a single generation. But, those times are all behind you; Afonso starts the game in poor health and in an (at the time) unnecessary regency under one of your many daughters. Historically, Afonso dies in 7 years, yet history may come quicker. You have seven children, three living sons and four living daughters, yet only one legitimate heir; Infanta Sancho, who would go on to be known as Sancho I 'the Populator.' Yet, Afonso's life is not over and his days of conquering may not be behind him yet; part of the de jure Portuguese kingdom is held by Moors invading the Iberian peninsula, and the southernmost province of modern Portugal is also held by the invaders. However, on top of this, Afonso holds an unpressed claim on the Kingdom of León. Either pressing this claim, or turning it into a pressed claim would solidify the Borgonha's control over both Portugal and Leon. Only your eldest daughter is married, leaving many roads for alliance open, along with a rich kingdom and coffers of gold for any conquest or ambition.

#### Aragon, Kingdom of

- **Alfonso de Barcelona, King of Aragon**: At last the quarrelling Jimena are contained in what remains of Navarre, but their legacy continues to poison the unity of the Iberian Catholics. Portugal, Castille, and Aragon remain divided even against the united Almohad Caliphate (Sultanate in-game). The House of Barcelona is surrounded by greater powers and has few friends. But you are Alfonso the Troubadour. Such a magnificent name demands a demesne to match. Perhaps even Gondar should owe fealty to Aragon...

### Orthodox

#### Byzantine Empire

- **Alexios Palaiologos, Duke of Bulgaria:** Alexios' family is distinguished even among the powerful and infuential names of the Byzantine Empire, having a seat at the poverbial table for over a century, and his ancestors contributed greatly to the Komnenos ascendancy. Their loyalty to the throne has been steadfast, but proximity to power often leads to powerful temptations: for how long will the Palaiologoi remain content to serve? Will Alexios and his family continue to suppor the throne, or will they covet it themselves?
- **Ioannes Kantakouzenos, Duke of Moesia:** One of the most seasoned of Basileus Manuel's commanders, Ioannes' herculean statue and bravery on the battlefield are equaled only by his astonishing arrogance and unbridled impetuosity, traits seldom conductive to a long and healthy life in the Byzantine political arena. Will Ioannes' boldness lead the Kantakouzenos family beond their present station, or will his arrogance ultimately prove to be their undoing?
- **Andronikos Angelos, Duke of Eprius:** A grandson of Basileus Alexios Komnenos, Andronikos has enjoyed a distinguished career in the service of the empire, but as more and more of his brothers and sons ascend the pyramid of power, the approach perilous heights at which a misstep could prove fatal to all. The Komnenos dyansty was built on the bonds of brothers, and you have an abundance of blood relations. Can you take advantage of your family ties to rise even higher?

#### Georgia

- **Tamar Bagrationi, Countess of Kakheti:** Despite the strident opposition of the Georgian nobility, the King of Georgia has made it clear that his eldest daughter, Tamar, will succeed him to the throne, an act unprecedented in the kingdom's lengthy history. The path forward will be difficult and few amogn her future vassals would be pleased to see her succeed, but those who know the princess well have every confidence that she will rise to the occasion. Can you prove those who doubt your worth wrong and lead Georgia to a glorious golden age?

### Apostolic

#### Anatolia

- **Levon Roupenid, Count of Seleucia:** Twice has death descended upon Levon's family: first it came for his father, a victim of Byzantine treachery, and then it returned for his uncle, a tyrannical apostate murdered by his own courtiers for fear that they would next experience his cruelties. Now Levon's older brother Roupen rules Cilicia, a man of saintly temperment presiding over a viperous nest of intrigue and ambition, and it may only be a matter of time before death strikes thrice. Will you be able to overcome your family's legacy of stife and make Cilicia a kingdom in its own right?

### Nestorian

#### Mjinjaa

- **Kutesk Uraqai, Duke of Uraqi**: In one of the furthest east provinces in the game sits an anomaly of a ruler. Belonging to the Öngüd Turkish tribe/culture, you rule the only province in the game of these people. Your people are known for being members of the Church of the East, or the Nestorian Church. You're currently a vassal of the Mjinjaa Kingdom, or Great Xia, a Taoist kingdom that lets you practice your Christianity in peace... for now. You're otherwise isolated from other Christians, save for a strip of Nestorians a ways to your north in Mongolia, with several of them beginning to feudalize. In the future, your people will be assimilated and forgotten by the Mongolian Empire, but it doesn't have to be this way. The future of the Nestorian Church and Christianity in the Eastern World lays with you, Kutesk.

#### Ötüken

- **Togrul Kereyid, High Chieftain of Övörkhangai:** The murder of his stepbrothers may have brought Togrul the chieftainship of hte Keraits, but it was only through his friend and ally Yesügei that he was able to retain it. Twice was Togrul driven into exile, and twice did Yesügei return him to power. But htere can be no third time, for Yesügei lies dead, and murdered by a rival tribe, and Togrul's enemies are all too aware of his protector's demise. Your foes circle like wolves, and where lies the strength to fend them all off?

#### Naiman Khanate

- **Inanch Bilge, Khan of Naiman Khanate:** Claiming mythical descedant from the Biblical Magi, Inach Bilge has proven his pedigree by wrestling independence from the Kara Khitai. But a grim fate awaits: the Khan's son and heir Tayang shall meet the mightly Temujin in battle, and fail. HIs wife Gübesu then ends her days as the victor's concubine...Can you challenge this destiny and ensure a strong succession? Or will your son's weakness doom your bloodlines to eternal shame?
