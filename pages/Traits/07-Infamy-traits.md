# Traits — Infamy traits


Infamy traits may be virtuous, accepted, shunned or considered criminal for a character depending on the Doctrines or Tenets of its rite. If either the character's rite or their liege's rite considers the trait shunned or criminal then the character will gain a secret instead of the trait, and will only gain the trait if the secret is exposed.
