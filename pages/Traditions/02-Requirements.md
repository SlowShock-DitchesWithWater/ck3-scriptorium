# Traditions — Requirements


Only the Culture Head can modify a culture's traditions. Some traditions do not appear in the menu unless some or all of the **Obligatory requirements** are fulfilled. Many traditions are mutually exclusive, the only way to bypass this restriction is through hybridization with a culture that has the other tradition, Although some are fundamentally incompatible and cannot be hybridized, these cases are mentioned in the Obligatory Requirements column.

### Prestige Cost

Adding a tradition *usually* has a base cost of  **2000** Prestige, although for some the cost is doubled (**4000**). There are many modifiers affecting a tradition's cost, they apply in the listed order:

- **+2000** if the culture does not have one of the Tradition's preferred ethos.
- **+3000** if the **optional requirement** is not fulfilled. This does not apply to AI Culture Heads.
- Explore Cultures discount (Kurultai Member)
- **−1000** to **−3000** Ritual Washing Specific
- **−50%** temporary event modifiers
- **+50%** if the tradition is replacing an existing tradition.
