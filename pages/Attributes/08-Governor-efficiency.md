# Attributes — Governor efficiency


|  | Available only with the Roads to Power DLC *or* the All Under Heaven DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Governor efficiency only appears on characters in a realm with  Administrative,  Celestial,  Ritsuryō,  Meritocratic or  Meritocratic Khanate government. It ranges from **-50%** to **+50%** and affects the domain's taxes and levies as well as the effectiveness of title men-at-arms by the same amount. Governor efficiency starts are **-100** and is increased by the following:

- **+1.7** per skill
- **+2** from having a level 4 education trait
- **+4** from having a level 5 education trait
- **+10** from Eunuch trait
- **+12** from Governor trait level 4
- **+9** from Governor trait level 3
- **+6** from Governor trait level 2
- **+3** from Governor trait level 1
- **+10%** from having a Private Study estate upgrade of at least level 2
- **-20** if the character is a vassal and owns titles that are not de jure part of the liege's primary title
