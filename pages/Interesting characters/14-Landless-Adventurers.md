# Interesting characters — Landless Adventurers


If you have the Roads to Power DLC, you will have access to Landless Adventurers. Adventurers are playable landless noble characters who travel the world with followers and a Camp, and have a different set of lifestyle perks. As they have no steady income from landed titles, adventurers primarily earn gold by completing contracts for landed rulers, and must stock up on provisions in order to move the camp.

- **Rodrigo the Campeador Vivar, of the Loyal Vassals:** Descended from noble lineage, Rodrigo enter the royal council at a very young age. At the service of Sancho II of Castile, Rodrgio trained in both courly and martial matters, under the careful watch of the king. However, Sancho's war against his brother Alfonso VI of Leon would lead to his premature death and Alfonso would then come to exile Rodrigo, indavertently satarting his legend. Will Rodrigo grow to become el Cid, "the lord", and emerage as a king in his own right, or will he remain a regular nobleman.
- **Hasan Sabbah, of the Summoners to the Faith:** Hasan Sabbah was born to a family of little renown, but his constant devotion to Islam and fanatical resistance to the Seljuk occupation would set him on a pather to alter history forever. From his humble origins, Hasan was destined to established the Assassins in their mountain keep of Alamut. He would become legend, and all would come to fear the dreaded Old Man of the Mountain. Do you have the guile and absolute faith to rally the devout and topple empires?
- **Hereweard the Wake Oslac, of the Men of the Fens:** Exiled and branded an outlaw by Edward the Confessor, Hereweard has traveled to Flanders to seek his fame and fortune. Hereweard would one day return to England and lead a determined resistance against the Norman invaders, becoming a folk hero of great renown. Will Hereweard remain a notorious villain under your guiding hand, or become the hero he was born to be?
- **Robert Phrangopoulos, of the Conquerors of Barbastro:** As a second son not standing to inherit, Robert Crispin has embraced the Norman spirit of adventure. Already a seasoned veteran of the conflict in Iberia, Robert has entered the employ of the Byzantine Emperor and now leads a contingent of Norman mercenaries to fight against the Turks. Will Robert find the glory he seeks as a great commander, or will he fall victim to the nest of vipers that is the Byzantine court?
- **Suleyman Seljuk, of the Sons of Qutalmish:** In the struggle for the Seljuk throne, Suleyman was defeated by his cousin, Alp Arslan, and driven into exile along his brothers. Humbled but not broken, he has managed to gain the allegiance of the local Turkman tribes in the Taurus Mountains. Suleyman would go on to carve out the Sultanate of Rum in the midst of the turmoil in Anatolia, and become embroiled in the wars of Byzantine pretenders. Will your strength be enough to lay the foundations of a new empire?
- **Wallada Umayyad, of the Thieves of Pleasure:** Daughter of the Caliph of Cordoba and one the last of the Umayyads, Wallada devoted her life to scholarship and learning, Caring little for the expectations of a woman in Andalusian society, Wallada walked unveiled through an austere world, shaprening the minds of women from all strata. Wallada's life is without borders, and her potential infinite. Adopt and train intelligent women from across the world to your cause. Can you break the confines of tradition and inherit the legacy of the Umayyad Caliphate?
- **Danismend Taylu, of the Seekers of Truth:** a Gazi of obsecure orgins, Danishmend Taylu's founded his dynasty's eponymous Beyik and after the 1071 Battle of Manizkert, which lasted for over a century. His dyansty insitally cooperated with the Seluks of Rum, but eventually fought them and were eventually rendered subservent to them. Can you reverse their their historical fortunes and assert them Danismend and his descendents and make them the Kings of Anatolia?
- **Ælfwine Haroldson Knytling,** **of the Peregrinatory Pilgrims:** The last surviving descendant of Canute the Great, who ruled over Denmark, Norway and England. Unlike his father and grandfather, he appeared to have no interest in ruling England and was content to serve as the Prior in southern French church. Can you lead this unambitious pilgrim follow the path of his forebears and restore your house to the throne of England? Or maybe even restore your ancestor's great realm?
- **Argyros Karatzas, of the Scythian Auxiliaries:** Though he is merely a landless adventurer, Argyros would eventually be appointed a *doux* in Byzantine Empire, and his desendants kept his dynasty influential for centuries: it's members included Ottoman officers, Wallachian Princes, and even a Patriarch of Constantinople. Can lead this humble adventurer's rise to prestige and influence?
- **Roger Raoul, of the Lordless Swords:** Initally a companion of Robert de Hauteville, Roger joined his fellow Norman in an attempted invasion of the Byzantine Empire but would end of betraying Robert by informing the Emperor about the invasion. He was subsequently awarded and continued to fight on behalf of the Empire until his death. The descendents of his brother, Raoul, continued serve the Empire even after the Fall of Constantinople in 1453. Will Roger once again defect to the Byzantines or remain faithful to the de Hautevilles? The decision is yours.
- **Song Jiāng, of the Liangshan Band:** Led a band of outlaws and eventually an armed rebellion against the Song Dynasty in China, but surrendered to the Imperial Court. He was later mentioned in the Chinese novel Water Margin.
