# Nickname — Unobtainable nicknames


The following nicknames cannot be obtained through a playable character, but can appear for other characters.

| Nickname | Good? | Requirement for NPC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| He/She, Fabulous He/She | Good | Court event, child of the ruler needs to be an unmarried (or polygamous) adult who is not a concubine and is either heterosexual or bisexual for an NPC with this to spawn Royal Court DLC |
| the Green | Good | Given to NPC characters involved in a certain event during travel |
| Son/Daughter of Wolves/Hyenas | ? | Travel event, a child with this nickname can appear and be taken in as a ward. The child will have a modifier along with possible certain congenital/physical traits (Physique/Intelligence groups or Strong/Dull). |
