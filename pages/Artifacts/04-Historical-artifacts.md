# Artifacts — Historical artifacts


Historical artifacts have mostly fixed effects. If they are lost or the required title does not exist in the chosen start date they may be discovered randomly through Adventure inspirations. If a character obtains an artifact it lacks the requirements to use only the Court Grandeur bonus will be given. Artifacts that need a certain religion can be used if the rite has the syncretism core tenet for the required religion.

### Bells of Santiago

|  | Available only with the Fate of Iberia DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

The Bells of Santiago are a trinket historical artifact that can only be used by characters Involved or Interloper in the Iberian Struggle. They have a unique mechanic where if a character who is Christian or Muslim or whose rite has the Christian Syncretism or Islamic Syncretism core tenet obtains the artifact but its religion cannot use it the artifact will be melted it into their own version of the artifact. If the artifact has been changed at least 4 times its description will start to keep track of the number of times it has been melted. The artifact is considered a holy relic and will give characters from the opposite religion a casus belli to recover them.

### Coronation artifacts

|  | Available only with the Coronations DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Every starting independent  King and above with the exception of the Byzantine, Holy Roman and Japanese emperors will start with an artifact. If the character is an  Emperor or their realm size is at least 60 the artifact will have **Famed** rarity. Otherwise it will have **Masterwork** rarity. The artifact will have the following effects regardless of rarity:

- **+8%** Legitimacy Gain
- **+0.02** Monthly Prestige
- One random effect

If the character's religion is Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Hausa, Finno-Ugric or Slavic the artifact will be a  Regalia. Otherwise it will be a  Crown.

If the  Royal Court DLC is also installed, when a ruler increases their title rank to  King or above for the first time, a courtier (always the  Antiquarian if one is employed) will gain an inspiration to create a coronation artifact.
