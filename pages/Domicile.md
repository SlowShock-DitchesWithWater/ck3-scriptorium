Domiciles are available to characters whose government type goes not require them to own landed titles. The appearance of buildings in a domicile depends on the character's culture and the terrain depends on the terrain of the barony where the domicile is located. There are six types of domiciles:

- Adventurer governments have a Camp
- Ecclesiastical governments have an Ecclesiastical estate
- Administrative governments have an Administrative estate
- Celestial, Meritocratic and Meritocratic Khanate governments have a Celestial estate
- Ritsuryō and Sōryō governments have a Manor
- Nomadic governments have a Nomadic capital
