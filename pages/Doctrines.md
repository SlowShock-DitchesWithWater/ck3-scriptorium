# Doctrines

> [!] This article has been verified for the current PC version (1.20) of the game.

Each rite has a large number of **doctrines**, which detail what is allowed and what is forbidden within a rite. They also affect how AI characters with the  Zealous trait will act. Doctrines that a character's faith doesn't know can be learned from events or the  Study Rite scheme.
