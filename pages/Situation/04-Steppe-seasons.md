# Situation — Steppe seasons


The Great Steppe regions before any expansion

|  | Available only with the Khans of the Steppe DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Steppe seasons are situations that affect characters with  Nomadic government present in certain locations if *The Great Steppe* Game Rule was enabled. The character with the highest  Herd involved in a situation will be considered the Gurkhan, who gains access to the  Unrestricted Dominance law and the Establish Paiza System decision.

There are 5 steppe season situations, depending on where the character's Nomadic capital is located. Every steppe season situation covers multiple regions, and different regions may be affected by different situation phases. The regions can be expanded via the Expand the Steppes decision.
