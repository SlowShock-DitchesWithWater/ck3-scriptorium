# Situation — Natural disasters


Natural disasters cannot happen in the first 10 years of a game. Afterwards, one of each natural disaster will take place every year. Selecting a disaster will display all affected counties with a flashing red border. Natural disasters are 2 times less likely to take place in the de jure capital of a  Duchy, and 4 times less likely if it's also the de jure capital of a  Kingdom. They are also 10 times less likely to take place in a region that already had a natural disaster.

Natural disaster situations affect the owners of counties where the disaster takes place, as well as any liege they have.

Every natural disaster has one of three levels of severity.

Every natural disaster situation has 3 phases.

1. **Warning** phases last 40 days; halfway through, every affected ruler will receive the option to gain 10 Stress and enter a regency while the situation lasts so that recovery contributions will cost 10% less. If the ruler is already in a regency the ruler will not gain Stress.
2. **Impact** phases last 30 days. Every affected ruler will lose 50 Legitimacy if not Hegemon of China and 100 Legitimacy if Hegemon of China and every location will lose Control, will no longer increase in Development, and will receive a modifier based on the situation's severity for 25 years. Every 7 days characters will die, every army located in the situation region has a 25-75% chance to be damaged and lose supplies (depending on its size), and the rulers affected will receive a random negative event. The map terrain will visibly change until the situation ends.
3. **Recovery** phases last for 15 years and give every affected ruler **-0.25** Monthly Legitimacy. Affected rulers can pay various contributions to receive bonuses. The cost of each contribution depends on the number of counties affected, the severity of the disaster, the ruler's Tax income and the innovations from their culture. If all mandatory contributions have been paid, the situation will end after another two months, and all penalties from the disaster will be removed. If the disaster affects multiple independent realms, each realm must pay the contributions separately to end the disaster within their own realm.

If a human player would be picked for death during a situation, both the player's character and a close family member, always the primary heir if there is one, will be at risk and the player will receive the following choices:

- Try to survive together through a twice as difficult Prowess challenge. Both characters will be wounded, and if the challenge is failed one of the characters will die.
- Try to survive separately through a Prowess challenge. Any character who fails the challenge will die.
- Sacrifice yourself to save the family member. The player character will die, the family member will escape unharmed, and the dynasty will gain 250 Renown.
- Sacrifice the family member to survive. The family member will die and the player character will escape unharmed; the player character will lose 350 Prestige and 250 Piety, and gain the Craven trait.

### Earthquake

Earthquakes affect an epicenter county and every adjacent county. An earthquake can take place anywhere in the world but is influenced by the following:

- Twice more likely if located in Asia Minor, China, Middle East, North-Eastern Africa, Indonesia, Japan, Philippines or Southern Europe
- Twice more likely if the county has at least one barony with Mountains or Desert Mountains terrain

### Flood

Floods can only take place in counties adjacent to a major river. The major river can be any in the world but it is twice more likely to cause a flood if any adjacent barony has  Farmlands or  Plains terrain.

Floods have the following levels of severity. They will always have a severity of 2 or 3 if the flooding river is Nile or Yellow River.

| Severity | Characters killed | Supply Limit | Construction Time | Monthly Control | Popular Opinion | Danger | Plague Resistance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | **5%** | **-60%** | **+60%** | **-0.6** | **-20** | **+20** | **-9** |
|  | **10%** | **-70%** | **+70%** | **-0.8** | **-25** | **+30** | **-12** |
|  | **15%** | **-80%** | **+80%** | **-1** | **-30** | **+40** | **-15** |

If all mandatory contributions are paid every county affected by the disaster will gain the  Good Fertility modifier for 10 years, which gives the following bonuses:

- **+25%** Supply Limit
- **+15%** Development Growth
- **+25%** County Fertility Growth
