# Rajas of Asia

> [!] Game modification
>
> [!] : Rajas of Asia

*Xin chào! Kumusta! Chax yip! Selamat! Suostei! Sabaidi! Minglarba! Sawasdee!*

**Rajas of Asia** massively extends the map to the far end of the Eurasian continent, from Champa to China, from Sulawesi to the Solomon Islands, featuring many new religions, cultures, events, decisions, governments, men-at-arms, buildings, traits, and more. Our overarching goal is to represent the region as deeply and accurately as possible, bringing the rich world of Asia into Crusader Kings 3.

◙════════════════════════════◙════════════════════════════◙

**Gameplay Changes and New Features:**

- Map expansion from the bitter cold of Sakhalin to the tropical hills of Papua New Guinea, from the monsoon jungles of Thailand to the warm beaches of Polynesia!
- A brand new 936 start date
- Total rework of the entire steppe map, including new branches of Tengriism and dozens of new cultures like Solqumyt, Kott, Shor, Basmyl, Qun, Uriankhai, Kerait, Merkit, Cik, and more!
- Full integration of and exclusive expansions to both Dharmachakra and Patlichan’s Caucasus Overhaul
- Suite of new governments with unique mechanics, including Mandala, Chinese Bureaucratic and Stateless, in addition to integrating Governments+ with its City-State and Nomadic governments.
- New tenets like Headhunting, Dhammavicaya and Spirit Guides
- Over a dozen new cultural traditions, including Angkorian Architecture, Tiger Sons, Warrior Caste, Migratory, Confucian Philosophy, Krabi Krabong, Thalassocratic, Disloyal Subjects, Soot-Faced Councils, Horses like Clouds, and more!
- Several kingdom and empire formation decisions for Nusantara and the Tai peoples
- The full breadth of medieval cultural and religious diversity represented by a stylish and expansive Minorities system. (Available separately as a standalone vanilla mod)
- Don’t think we left vanilla out to dry - Exclusive flavor for *all* of Asia, from Cappadocia to the Caucasus and Burma to Bjarmaland!
