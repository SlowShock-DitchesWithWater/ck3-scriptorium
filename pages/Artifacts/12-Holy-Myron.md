# Artifacts — Holy Myron


|  | Available only with the By God Alone DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Holy Myron are trinket artifacts that can be created by the Prepare the Holy Myron decision. The artifact rarity depends on how much the character paid when it took the decision and the  Learning of the religious affairs councilor.

The artifact has up to 4 charges, which can be spent on the Anoint an Artifact with Holy Myron decision. Charges can also be gifted to another adult ruler whose religion is  Christianity, which will give the said ruler a  **Common** variant of the artifact. Once all charges are used the artifact is removed.

The artifacts have the following effects:

| Rarity | Monthly Piety | Clergy Opinion | Available uses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Common** | 0.1 | 5 | 1 |
| **Masterwork** | 0.1 | 5 | 2 |
| **Famed** | 0.2 | 5 | 3 |
| **Illustrious** | 0.2 | 5 | 4 |
