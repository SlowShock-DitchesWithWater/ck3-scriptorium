# Interesting characters — Pagan characters


### Ásatrú

#### Aquitaine

- **Hæsteinn Hæsteining, Count of Montaigu**: Through cunning and courage, Hæsteinn has become a rich man. After leading a lucrative raid through the Mediterranean together with Björn *Ironside*, Hæsteinn has settled in Brittany. The Franks have coffers ready for picking, and a mighty Viking warrior makes a great ally in times of political turmoil. Fortune does not come without enemies, however. Will Hæsteinn's greed get the better of him, or will his dynasty prosper under your guidance?

#### Brittany

- **Olafr af Brest, Count of Léon**: Olafr af Brest represents the Norse raiders who seized parts of Brittany in the 9th and 10th centuries. Historically, the Vikings established their rule over Brittany between 907 and 939, driving the Breton nobility into exile and ending the recently founded Kingdom of Brittany. Pressured by the Franks, they eventually converted to Catholicism and formed the Kingdom of Bretland, with Nantes (renamed to Namsborg) as their capital. Their rule ended when a Breton uprising, supported by Frankish nobles, King Athelstan of England, and Duke William of Normandy, and led by the exiled Breton prince Alan, grandson of King Alan the Great, restored the independent Duchy of Brittany. During their short reign, the Norse ravaged the Loire region, Burgundy, and even planned invasions of England before being driven out. As Olafr, you can change history: secure permanent Norse rule over Brittany, build a mighty raiding kingdom to terrorize France, England, and Iberia, or use Brittany as a base for further conquest into England or Francia. You may even choose to adopt Catholicism and found a Norse state of raiding crusaders, striking deep into Islamic Iberia, North Africa, and even Arabia. The future of these mighty Vikings is yours to shape—will you lead them to eternal glory, or let their legacy be forgotten?

#### Denmark

- **Sigurdr 'Snake-in-the-Eye' af Sigurdr, Jarl (High Chieftain) of Sjælland**: As his brothers sailed off to England to exact their vengeance, Sigurdr *Snake-in-the-Eye* stayed behind to rule his late father's lands. Ragnarr *Loðbrók* has left him some very large boots to fill. Sigurdr's descendants will be Kings of Denmark, Norway and England for well over a century. Can you fulfill the destiny of his dynasty?
- **Holmger Hammer, Chieftain (Count) of Bornholm**: Officially has the best dynasty name in the game.
- **Bagsecg Eirikrsson Jute, Jarl (Duke) of Jylland**: Bagsecg Jute of Jylland stands as a mighty Norse warlord of the Viking Age. Though his early life is shrouded in mystery, Bagsecg is remembered as one of the leaders of the Great Heathen Army that invaded England. According to the Anglo-Saxon Chronicle, he and Halfdan Hvitserk led Viking forces into Wessex, where Bagsecg met his end at the Battle of Ashdown in 871. In 867, however, his fate is still unwritten. From his stronghold in Jylland, Bagsecg is well-positioned to unite the Danes under one banner and crown himself King of Denmark. From there, he could lead his warriors across the North Sea, joining the Great Heathen Army andperhaps even claiming the English throne for himself. Bagsecg’s legacy is one of ambition, conquest, and glorious death in battle. Will you honour his name by forging a dynasty that endures for centuries, or will your legacy be lost to the mists of history?

#### England

- **Halfdan 'Whiteshirt' Hvitserk, Jarl (Duke) of Jórvík**: The first wave of the invasion has gone very well for Halfdan *Whiteshirt*. With the help of his brother Ivar, he has wrestled the control of York from King Ælla's hands. And he will take so much more. Will you follow in history's footsteps and have your bloody vengeance, or stake out a new path for Halfdan?

#### Iceland

- **Garðar Svavarsson, Chieftain (Count) of Austrland**: Historically, Garðar was a Swede who briefly resided in Iceland. He is said to be the second Scandinavian to reach the island and the first known person to circumnavigate it. Can one man (literally) build an empire from the far corner of the world?

#### The Isles

- **Ivar 'the Boneless' Ivaring, Jarl (High Chieftain) of the Suðreyjar**: Rumors say that Ivar is weak of body, but no one can claim he is weak of mind. Through cunning and confidence, he has united a great number of Norse warbands under his banner, and won great victories on these foreign shores. But not even the greatest Viking can cheat death. In 870, Ivar will fade from history forever... Can you win Ivar's wars, and keep him alive to rule for many years to come?
- **Ketill Björnsson Flatnefr, Chieftain (Count) of the Mön**: The father of Auðr *the Deep-Minded* and grandfather of Þorsteinn *the Red*, who would briefly rule half of Scotland. You start as a vassal of Ivar *the Boneless*, who has a few thousand event troops under his command, so gaining independence will be difficult. Your daughter has already betrayed the Old Gods and fled to Iceland, but you won't give up so easily. Will you try to expand your domain by conquering Ireland, where your son-in-law failed, or will your choice fall on the many little lords in Wales? Who knows? Do you have what it takes to become the true King of all the Isles?

#### Lotharingia

- **Hroðulfr Haraldrsson Akra, Count of Ostfriesland**: You are the only pagan vassal in the Kingdom of Lotharingia. Your uncle, Hrörekr, is, as the Duke of Holland, your liege. Hrörekr has betrayed the Old Gods and is already a Catholic. They call you the black sheep of the family. Can you show them what you are worth?

#### Norway

- **Haraldr 'Tanglehair' Yngling, Chieftain (Count) of Vestfold**: Play as Haraldr *Fairhair* who is considered to be the man who unified the petty kingdoms of Norway into one. Haraldr *Fairhair* was the first King of Norway, which he united to marry the woman he loved (the daughter of Eirikr Horðalandi, who is in-game as Gyda Eiríkrsdatter, the only Norwegian in 867); that is what the sagas tell. Take the challenge yourself and see if you can follow the sagas of the Norse king who would not shave his hair until he ruled over Norway.
- **Grjotgard Lade, Chieftain (Count) of Naumdælafylki**: The Jarls of Lade ruled over all the lands of Hálogaland and Þrændalög. They allied Haraldr *Fairhair* and remained the true power in Northern Norway. During the later conflicts with Denmark over the Norwegian crown, the Jarls of Lade would play kingmakers. Can you do better?

#### Novgorod

- **Rurik 'Troublemaker' Rurikid, Jarl (High Chieftain) of Holmgarðr (Novgorod)**: Few have risen from nothingness to greatness like the brave Rurik! Together with his brothers, he brought order to the Eastern wilderness, and since their deaths, he is the lone ruler of a great chiefdom. Yet, this is only the beginning. The vast lands of Russia and the Baltic are open for conquest, and there are many paths you could take. One day, Rurik's descendants will found the Tsardom of Russia. Or will they? Will your dynasty stay true to your Nordic roots and unite your homeland, or will you or your descendants fall sway to the preaching of the Perun worshipers (as your son and heir has) or even the Christ-God of Miklagarðr? Play as the famed Varangian chieftain and begin your journey to unite the Slavic lands of Russia. The destiny of this great dynasty rests in your hands.

#### Ruthenia

- **Dyre 'the Stranger' Oskyldr, Jarl (High Chieftain) of Könugarðr (Kyiv)**: The first Norse ruler of Kyiv remains a mystery to historians. The Norse coming from Holmgarðr settled Könugarðr to have better access to the Black Sea and the riches of Miklagarðr (Constantinople). Historically, Askold and Dir were slain by Helgi *the Seer*, son of Rurik *Troublemaker*, and Kyiv would become the center of the Ruthenia territory whose rulers would go on to raid Constantinople before converting to Orthodoxy. You start as a disinherited bastard son of Halfdan *Whiteshirt*, but you are also a genius with great stats. The vast lands of Russia and the Khazaria (especially after the Magyars settle) are open for conquest, and there are many paths you could take. A river adjacent to the capital allows access to the Black and Mediterranean seas, which makes Crimea, Krete, and Cyprus easy pickings.

#### Sweden

- **Björn 'Ironside' af Munsö, Jarl (High Chieftain) of Uppland**: Björn has lived a true Norseman's life of adventure. A fearsome raider, he has pillaged across France, Iberia, and Italy, together with his friend Hæsteinn of Montaigu. Now, the forces of Scandinavia gather to assault British shores once more. Though Björn is promised to avenge the death of his father in England, his descendants will rule Sweden for the next two centuries. On which side of the North Sea does your destiny lie?
- **Rikulfr Hildursson Ulfing, Chieftain (Count) of Östergötland**: Not Beowulf's dynasty, but a member of a dynasty who makes an appearance in the saga. Still, one of the only two Geatish dynasties in the game (along with the House Gautske).
- **Guðmundr Nerking, Chieftain (Count) of Neeric**: Guðmundr Nerking is a vassal under the great Björn Ironside. In an age of adventure, conquest, and glory, many Norsemen have turned west in search of riches, ravaging England and France, slowly forgetting the untamed tribes across the Baltic Sea. This is your perfect opportunity. Break free from the grasp of Björn Ironside before he becomes King of Sweden and lead a great Varangian adventure eastward. Cross the Baltic, settle in Estonia, Lithuania, Prussia, or Pomerania, and forge a mighty brotherhood of warriors, the legendary Jomsvikings, to terrorise the local Slavs, Balts, and even the Catholic Germans. In the golden age of the Norse, great riches await those bold enough to seize them. Will you go down in history as a great conqueror and Viking King of the Baltics, or be forgotten as a mere footnote in the sagas of legendary kings?
- **Sverker Tykesson Stenbock, Chieftain (Count) of Ångermanland**: Sverker Stenbock is an independent Norse chieftain in northern Sweden. With Sweden on the verge of unification under Björn Ironside, son of the legendary Ragnarr *Loðbrók*, the independence of your holdings is in grave danger. Fighting the much stronger King of the Swedes seems hopeless, but a new hope awaits across the sea. Sail across the Gulf of Bothnia and settle in the lands of the Finns or the Sami, carving out a new realm far from Swedish control. There, you can become a mighty Viking lord of the east, centuries before the Swedes would historically conquer Finland. Once crowned a king, you may even turn your strength toward the Baltic tribes of Estonia, the Ugric tribes of Bjarmaland or even against the Swedish kingdom that once threatened your freedom. Survival in the age of conquerors is harsh, but for those who prevail, fame, glory, and wealth await. Will you rise as one of the great conquerors, or will your lands fall to your enemies and your legacy be forgotten?

### Akom

#### Akan

- **Maaman Kwakou, Krontihene (Duke) of Eweland**: As Krontihene of Eweland, he is well-positioned to claim two of the Holy Sites of the Akom Pagan faith, located in Bono and Kumasi. From there, he can work to unite the Guan, Akan, and Kru peoples, who share his cultural heritage, and form a strong kingdom capable of defending itself against the impending Islamic incursions that will soon convert much of the region. Once this kingdom is established, it will be easier to conquer the Ewe, Yoruba, Edo, and Igbo peoples of the Niger Delta and reclaim the Akom Holy Sites of Nikki and Ife. By securing three of these sacred sites, he can reform the Akom faith, repel the Islamic invaders, and potentially reverse the Islamic conversion by advancing into North Africa and converting it to his religion instead.

### Bidaic

#### Ghana

- **Zoumana Cisse, Maghan (King) of Ghana**: As the most powerful ruler in Sub-Saharan Africa, Zoumana Cisse begins in an enviable position, already controlling one Bidaic Holy Site and a location for a gold mine. From here, he can swiftly conquer his southern and northern neighbours to claim the additional gold mines of Mali and the salt mines of Ijil and Taghaza, rapidly amassing vast wealth. During these conquests, it is likely he will also gain control of two more Holy Sites, enabling him to reform the Bidaic faith and establish a strong religious foundation for his people. Historically, the Cisse dynasty of the Ghana Empire succumbed to Almoravid influence and converted to Islam over 200 years later, in 1076. However, by reforming the Bidaic faith early, you may alter their destiny, expanding their influence to the Mediterranean and subjugating the Berbers rather than adopting their ways.

#### Songhay

- **Za Kuroy Zuwa, Nyanyagume (Duke) of Kawkaw**: He begins in a challenging position on the edge of the powerful Ghana Kingdom. A follower of the Bidaic faith, he claims descent from Alayaman, a legendary figure said to have come from Yemen. His first goal will be to unify the Songhai people and establish a strong kingdom in that region. From there, the gold mines of Mali, controlled by Ghana, present an ambitious opportunity. By converting to Ibadi Islam, he could ally with the Berbers of North Africa to gain the strength needed to challenge Ghana's status as dominant power in the region and claim their wealth for himself. Once he has built a prosperous empire with the Gold from his new mines, he might even turn on his former Berber allies, expanding his influence into the Sahara and the Mediterranean.

#### Hausaland

- **Nyalgeh Mossi, Debegume (Count) of Tahoua**: Nyalgeh Mossi represents the early rise of the Mossi people of West Africa, destined to forge the powerful Mossi Kingdoms that would dominate the region until the 19th century. In 867, the Mossi are still in their formative stage, but their central position allows for ambitious expansion into Hausaland, Kanem, Songhay, and the Gur territories. Historically, the Mossi carved out their legacy through skilled cavalry warfare and resilient leadership. As a follower of the traditional Bidaic faith, you may choose to consolidate your power, capture sacred sites, and reform the religion to resist the growing tide of Islam from the north. Alternatively, embrace Islam and establish a Mossi Sultanate in the heart of the Sahel. The Mossi have a history rich in glory and strength—will you build their empire ahead of time, securing centuries of dominance, or fall before your rivals can even remember your name?

#### Jenne

- **Junkunda Sorogoye, Debegume (Count) of Bindugu**: Junkunda Sorogoye holds an important position along the fertile banks of the Niger River. As a member of the Bozo people, renowned as the "masters of the river," your maritime expertise and rich lands offer a strategic advantage in the harsh climate of West Africa. Use this opportunity to unite the Bozo clans under the Sorogoye banner and found the mighty Kingdom of Jenne, ensuring stability for your people and creating new opportunities for conquest. Establish a powerful Bozo empire centred around the Niger River, and perhaps even seize control of the nearby gold mines of Mali to secure immense wealth and influence. Despite this great position, challenges loom on the horizon. The rise of Islam across West Africa presents both threats and opportunities. As proud followers of the Bidaic faith, you must choose: retain your ancient traditions and stand as a bastion of African heritage, or embrace the new Islamic faith to strengthen alliances and expand your power. Whatever path you take, you can transform Jenne into a beacon of African civilisation, the destiny of the Bozo people and the riches of the Niger are yours to command.

### Bori

#### Hausaland

- **Daurama Daura, Petty Magajiya (Chieftess) of Daura**: As the Last Magajiya of Daura, Daurama is a symbol of the old ways of the Hausa people. Her new husband, Bayajidda *the Slayer of the Snake* (originally from Iraq), represents change, as more and more outsiders flock to Hausaland, bringing their strange faiths and cultures with them... Daurama is set to go down in history as the Last Magajiva, but does she have to be?

#### Kanem

- **Labidiedo Kirdi, Chima (Count) of Fika**: Labidiedo Kirdi rules the County of Fika, a small and isolated realm far from the great centres of power. As a member of the Sao people and a follower of the ancient Bori faith, you stand at the edge of history, despite this, opportunity lies within reach. Begin by forging a new culture for your people, setting the stage for greatness and unlocking the "Nobody Comes to Fika" achievement, itself a nod to the Swedish custom of Fika. From there, conquer the minor chieftains surrounding your lands, secure your own kingdom, and spread your new culture across the region to establish true cultural domination. In matters of faith, you may choose to reform the ancient Bori traditions and preserve your people's heritage, or convert to the nearby Coptic faith of Abyssinia to forge powerful alliances and gain new legitimacy. Despite your humble beginnings, Labidiedo of Fika has the chance to defy the odds and reshape the fate of West Africa. Let them say nobody comes to Fika, soon they will all come to kneel before you.

### Donyi-Poloist

#### Lhomon

- **Pelri Dzayul, High Chieftain (Duke) of Dzayül**: Already holds 2 Holy Sites of his faith, the third one is located on a bordering county. A not too hard start to experiment with the Reform Faith mechanic.

### Kiratist

#### Himalaya

- **Mabo Hang Lasahang, Prabhu (Duke) of Limbuwan**: Mabo Hang Lasahang rules the mountainous region of Limbuwan in the eastern Himalayas. Revered as a reincarnation of God, he leads the Kirati people under the banner of Kiratism, an unreformed pagan faith whose Holy Sites lie scattered across the Himalayas, controlled by powerful Hindu dynasties like the Katyuri and Licchavi. Around 870, Mabo Hang historically faced rebellion from his vassal, Na Hang of Daramdin, a follower of Yumaist Kiratism. His decisive victory over the rebels led to the banishment of the Chilikchom people, cementing his rule. Will you rise against these mightier foes, vanquish your rebellious vassals, reclaim the Holy Sites of your faith, and reform Kiratism with yourself as its divine leader? Lead the Kirati to glory and eternal fame, cementing the Lasahang dynasty as a major regional power. The destiny of Kiratism and Limbuwan lies in your hands.

### Kushite

#### Blemmyia

- **Yin ibn Zanafig Kelou, Emir (Duke) of Bazin**: Yin ibn Zanafig governs the High Chiefdom of Bazin, a tribal stronghold of the ancient Kushite faith, venerating Wepwawet and the gods of the regional pantheon. Bazin stands as one of the final bastions of Kushite tradition, alongside the neighboring High Chiefdom of Baqlin and the distant Daju lands of Kordofan. Unite the Beja tribes under your banner and form the Kingdom of Blemmyia to repel the encroaching Islamic powers. From there, reclaim your Holy Sites and reform the Kushite faith, spreading the worship of the old gods as far as Egypt, restoring their glory to the land of Kemet. Alternatively, head south into Abyssinia, adopt Coptic Christianity, and rise as the Beja rulers of a revitalized Aksumite Empire. The future of Bazin and its ancient legacy is in your hands.
- **Sinder Amarar, Emir (Duke) of Baqlin**: Sinder Amarar, rules over the the High Chiefdom of Baqlin, one of the last Beja lands still loyal to the old Kushite gods. To the north, the Emir of Naqis has already embraced Islam, but the southern Beja remain free. Stand with the Emir of Bazin, a potential ally, to defend your faith, unite the Beja tribes, and form the Kingdom of Blemmyia by reclaiming Naqis and driving out foreign rule. Or turn to your Christian neighbors in Makuria, Alodia, and Abyssinia, adopt the Coptic faith, and lead the Beja as a new Christian power in the Nile Valley. The future of Baqlin and the Beja people is yours to shape.

### Melie

#### Guiyi

- **Lang Tuoba, Duke of Sogo Khoto**: Lang Tuoba, a scion of the ancient Tuoba clan and vassal of the Han military governor Huaishen Zhang of Guiyi, rules Sogo Khoto. Descended from the Xianbei founders of the Northern Wei dynasty which ruled much of northern China during the during the 4th and 5th centuries, Lang may attempt to restore his lineage’s glory. As a member of the Tuyuhun culture and a follower of the Melie faith, Lang governs a land rich in tradition but surrounded by rivals. By conquering Xia, Lang can secure the Holy Sites needed to reform the Melie faith and revive its spiritual power. From there, he could turn to the lands of Amdo and Gyalrong, liberating the Tuyuhun from Tibetan rule and building a state worthy of his ancestors.

### Orisan

#### Yorubaland

- **Ila Oduduwa, Ajapada (Duke) of Ife**: Ila Oduduwa, ruler of Ife, claims descent from Oduduwa, the divine creator of the Yoruba people. Can you unite the Yoruba, forge the Kingdom of Yorubaland, and conquer neighboring tribes? Perhaps you will go further, claiming the Holy Sites of Bono and Kumasi deep in Akan territory to reform the Orisan faith and solidify your status as its spiritual leader. Assert your divine lineage and build a mighty West African empire, leaving a legacy worthy of the gods.

### Roogan

#### Kaabu

- **Lamane Serer, Geer (Count) of Saalum**: Lamane Serer rules Saalum, a core region of the Wolof people in present-day Senegal, renowned for fiercely resisting Islamic incursions to preserve their ancient traditions. With rising Islamic influence from the northern Berbers, Lamane is poised to unify the Wolof and Pulaar peoples and forge the Kingdoms of Kaabu and Takrur. From this strong base, you can march north to challenge the Berbers and reclaim the Holy Site of Wadan, or expand south to conquer Bolm and Falanko, reforming the Roog Sene faith. Establishing religious dominance will solidify your realm as a major power in West Africa, capable of resisting Islamic expansion and forging a mighty Senegambian Empire.

### Sedist

#### Kamarupa

- **Ayangba Ningthouja, Count of Manipur**: Ayangba Ningthouja rules Manipur, the heart of a dynasty that has governed these lands since 33 AD and endured until the unification of India. Here, Ayangba is of Burmese culture and follows the ancient Sedist faith, with its Holy Sites located in the region of Lhomon. In later centuries, the dynasty would adopt Hinduism, blending it with local traditions. Ayangba can expand his influence by uniting the surrounding realms, conquering the Kingdoms of Lhomon and Kamarupa. By reclaiming Sedist Holy Sites and reforming the faith, he could establish a Sedist Empire and secure his dynasty’s dominance in northern India.

### Shintō

#### Japan

- **Kōno Fukami**: One of several unlanded characters in Japan, Fukami is unique in that his manor is not in Kyōto, but rather in Iyo. Historically, the Kōno clan were a powerful family in Iyo province for centuries, eventually becoming governors of the province at the start of the Muromachi period. However, the Kōno have no special obligation to Iyo: with a skillful hand, the Kōno can either come to dominate the title of Kampaku, or establish a Sōryō government in one of the provinces and challenge the old order in its entirety.

### Siguic

#### Mali

- **Bamari Keita, Farbas (Duke) of Manding**: Bamari Keita leads the Manding region, the ancestral heartland of the Malinke people and the birthplace of the Keita dynasty. Later claiming descent from Bilal ibn Rabah, a companion of the Prophet Muhammad, the Keitas historically united the Malinke chiefdoms, overtook the Ghana Empire, founded the Mali Empire in the 13th century and later converted to Islam, eventually rising to global prominence under rulers like Mansa Musa, whose 14th-century reign made Mali one of the wealthiest realms in the world. Can you emulate this legendary rise to power, conquering the Ghana region and forming a mighty empire even faster than historically? With skillful leadership, you can establish Mali as a dominant Islamic powerhouse, renowned for its wealth and influence across West Africa and beyond.

#### Sahara

- **Isli ibn Tacfin Kel Wi, Sheikh (Count) of Takkeda**: Isli ibn Tacfin, a Butr Tuareg ruler of the Kel Ayr, governs Takedda, a settlement poised to become a key hub for copper mining and trans-Saharan trade. Historically, the Kel Ayr played a significant role in shaping the politics of the region, notably pushing back the Hausa and other southern states during the 11th century, before eventually being integrated into the Songhai Empire in the 16th century. Surrounded by the Zaghawa, the Kingdom of Kanem to the east, and the Hausa to the south, Isli stands at the crossroads of conquest and faith. Leading the Kel Ayr Tuaregs, you can expel the Kanuki rulers of Kanem and establish dominance from Takedda to Darfur, ruling over the Zaghawa population. From there you can either work towards preserving the Siduic faith and resist foreign influence, embrace Islam to secure alliances and avoid Berber invasions, or expand further into Darfur to adopt Coptic Christianity through contact with Alodia. The destiny of the Kel Ayr and the desert empire they might build rests in your hands.

### Slovianskan

#### Bohemia

- **Neklan Přemyslid, Duke of Bohemia**: Neklan, the sixth of the seven mythical princes of Bohemia, is remembered as the grandfather of Bořivoj I, the first historical Přemyslid ruler. In 867, pagan Bohemia is surrounded by powerful Catholic neighbors like the Franks and Great Moravia. With its rich silver mines, Bohemia has the potential to become one of Europe’s wealthiest polities. By converting to Catholicism, you can forge strong alliances, expand into Pagan Polabian and Polish lands, and take over Great Moravia through marriage or conquest. Unite the western Slavs and forge an independent Bohemian Empire, securing Czech dominance in the heart of Europe.

#### Bosnia

- **Hvalimir Krajinić Belojevic, Count of Rama**: Hvalimir rules the county of Rama, a key territory within the First Serbian Principality, with strong ties to the ruling Vlastimirović dynasty through his mother. Hvalimir is in a prime position to expand his holdings by conquering the nearby regions of Zachlumia, Travunia, and Duklja. Once these lands are secured, he will find himself as Serbia's most powerful vassal, with the ability to shape the realm’s future as a loyal Kingmaker, securing himself feudal privileges enabling his family to grow in might and influence. Alternatively, Hvalimir might use this new power to overthrow his liege and seize Serbia for himself. Converting to Christianity at this point would safeguard his realm from Holy Wars and open the door to alliances with neighboring powers. From there, he could expand into Bulgarian, Greek, and Albanian lands to establish a Serbian Empire akin to the one that emerged in the 14th century. Will you be a loyal vassal or will your take the throne of Serbia for yourself? The choice is yours to make.

#### Poland

- **Siemowit Piast, High Chieftain (Duke) of Polania**: The first semi-historical Duke of the Polans and son of Piast *the Wheelwright*, who is believed to be the founder of the Polish state. Consolidate the Polish and Pomeranian tribes and then take the battle to the Baltic pagans to your north-east to form West-Slavia. Will you Christianize your people, or unite all the Slavs under a reformed pagan faith?

#### Sorbia

- **Jaromir Lusici, Chieftain (Count) of Błota**: Jaromir Lusici leads the Lusici tribe of the Lusatian Sorbs in the region of Spreewald. Their neighbor, the powerful Milczanów tribe, controls Lusatia and Milcenia, offering the perfect opportunity to swear fealty, grow your strength from within, and one day seize their lands to unite the Sorbs under your rule. Crown yourself King of all the Sorbs and forge a distinct, united Sorbian culture and identity. But beware, the Karlings in the west will not welcome your rising pagan kingdom. You may choose to embrace Catholicism to avoid German conquest and forge strong alliances, perhaps even founding a Sorbian Holy Roman Empire. Or defy history by remaining loyal to the old Slavic gods and lead a Slovianskan revival across Sorbia, Pomerania, Poland, Ruthenia, and even into Germany, reforming your faith and building a united West Slavic realm. Historically, the Sorbs fell under German rule within the following century, now you have the chance to change their fate. Will you preserve their independence and secure their place in Europe, or will they be conquered by their powerful neighbors and forgotten by history?

#### Serbia

- **Mutimir Vlastimirić Vlastimirovic, Duke of Raška**: Mutimir Vlastimirović, Duke of Raška, is a powerful Serbian ruler in the 9th century who helped lay the foundations of a united and Christian Serbia. During his reign, he famously defeated an invasion by Boris I of Bulgaria, securing peace and stability in the region. His rule also marked the beginning of closer ties with the Byzantine Empire and the slow Christianisation of the Serbs. In 867, Mutimir is still a follower of the old Slavic gods, but his sons have already embraced Christianity. Following in the steps of your sons and converting to Orthodoxy can give you the religious authority to conqer the smaller Serbian chieftains and crown yourself King of the Serbs. From there, you can choose your path. Remain independent and ally with Bulgaria to challenge Byzantine power and create a Serbian Empire long before its time. Alternatively, swear fealty to the Byzantine Emperor and become a powerful vassal on the western front of the Empire, conquering Bulgarian lands to weaken the enemies of your Byzantine liege and defending Constantinople against those who seek to destroy it. Can you forge a lasting Serbian kingdom or will your legacy fade away, overshadowed by your mighty neighbours?

### Táltosist

#### Mogyër Confederation

- **Álmos Ügyekfi Árpád, Grand Prince (King) of the Mogyër Confederation**: When the seven Hungarian tribes united to elect a supreme leader, their votes fell on brave Álmos. One day, his son Árpád will pick up the mantle — but only if the Hungarians can keep their independence. Danger looms on the Eastern horizon. Can you defend the Hungarians and lead them to greatness? This is your chance to retell the adventures of the Árpád dynasty!

### Tengri

#### Bulgaria

- **Baghatur Khunzakhal, Duke of Visegrad**: Your lands are the last remnant of the Avar Khaganate, the most feared power in the region some 200 years earlier. Reduced to a mere vassal of the Orthodox Bulgars, you can take advantage of the Magyar invasion to secure your independence. Then outrace and outfight the new invaders to dominate the Pannonian Basin and reform the khaganate.

#### Khazaria

- **Shilki Dulo, High Chieftain (Duke) of Volga-Bulgaria**: Shilki Dulo rules Volga-Bulgaria from Bolghar as a vassal of the Khazar Khaganate.
