# Attributes — Candidate Score


|  | Available only with the Roads to Power DLC *or* the All Under Heaven DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

  - *Main article: Succession_laws#Candidate_succession*

Certain governments use a candidate-based succession, where every character eligible has a **Candidate Score**. When the succession happens the title goes to the character with the highest Candidate Score, and the character's Candidate Score goes back to 0. If the government type also uses  Merit, Merit Rank weights more than Candidate Score.
