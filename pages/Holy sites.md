# Holy sites

> [!] This article has been verified for the current PC version (1.20) of the game.

Holy sites

Holy sites are baronies that are considered of religious significance by a faith and provide bonuses if held by a character of their faith. Most faiths start with 5 holy sites and can have a maximum of 9. When reforming or creating a new faith, the new faith will inherit the holy sites of the old faith. Rulers can, remove and change the status of holy sites in their realm as well as adopt holy sites that belong to other faiths if they meet one of the following conditions:

- If faith has a head of faith the ruler has them as a puppet
- If faith does not have a head of faith the ruler must have the Paragon of Virtue level of devotion or above and either a Realm Priest Puppet, Ecclesiastical government or Theocratic government
- Heads of faith can also remove and change the status of holy sites of their faith and do not need them in domain to do so.

Adopting and removing holy sites costs 1000  Piety. Removing a holy sites also gives **-20**  Opinion with every  Zealot and  Parochial vassal of the same faith for 10 years, and if the holy site had an owner it also gives **-50**  Opinion with them for 10 years. To adopt a holy site, the faith must not have another holy site in the de jure lands of the same kingdom and the holy site must belong to the same religion unless the rite has the syncretism core tenet matching the religion of the holy site's faith or has the Adaptability or Adoptionism tenet and is the mainline rite. If a barony with a special building is a holy site for a faith then all religion restrictions on its special building are removed.

On the map holy sites will glow red if they're controlled by rulers of a different faith and yellow if they're controlled by rulers of the same faith but have no enshrined relics.
