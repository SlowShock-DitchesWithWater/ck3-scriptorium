# Interesting characters — Buddhist Characters


### Mahayana

#### Khotan

- **Wolila Xiao, Chieftain of Emil:** Wolila had two strong sons. The first, steadfast Doulubu, the Pacifier of the East, married the Empress of Kara Khitai. The second, dishonest Puguzi, became her lover. Wife and brother: together they plotted the death of Duolubu. Enraged by their fratricide, Wolila brought the lovers to justice, to death. Zhilugu of the Yelü then found his way onto the throne of the Kara Khitai. The last storm is coming of the Khitans. Can you speak for your people, stand for your honor once again, and change their fate?
