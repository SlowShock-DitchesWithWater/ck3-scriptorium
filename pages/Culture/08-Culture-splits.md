# Culture — Culture splits


The following cultures will divide into multiple small cultures once certain time periods are reached. A player character will get the option to cancel the culture split if they controls 80% of the counties with the original culture.

| Original Culture | Period | Region | New Cultures | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anglo-Saxon | 867-950 | Scotland | Scots | County is in Scotland |
| Norse | 950-990 | Northern Europe | Danish | County is in Denmark |
| Norse | 950-990 | Northern Europe | Norwegian | County is in Norway |
| Norse | 950-990 | Northern Europe | Swedish | County is in Northern Europe |
