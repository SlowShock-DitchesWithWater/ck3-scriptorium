# Interesting characters — Hindu Characters


### Shaivism

**Rajputana**

- **Prithviraja III Chauhan, Maharaja of the Chauhan Kingdom:** An important character in Indian folklore, until this day. He managed to forge a legendary alliance and fended off the Islamic general Ghori in 1191 during the first battle of Tarain. The second battle of Tarain in 1192, however, was lost by Chauhan, which led to his execution.
