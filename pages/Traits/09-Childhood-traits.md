# Traits — Childhood traits


Characters between 3 and 15 years old will have a childhood trait, which is decided at random but the chance can be influenced by a few cultural traditions. Childhood traits grant a minor bonus to two Skills and if the child is given an Education Focus that matches one of the two Skills the trait improves, then the trait can improve the level of the education trait given when the character reaches adulthood. If creating a custom ruler each childhood trait costs 5 customization points.
