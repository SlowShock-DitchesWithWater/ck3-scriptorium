*"Driven by religious fervor and the promise of rewards both spiritual and temporal, princes and paupers alike marched eastward in the name of God, gold, and everlasting glory.*

***"This is the age of the crusades, at the crossroads of religious zeal and worldly ambition. Choose your path wisely, for what prize is greater than the Holy Land itself?"***

*The seemingly endless hoofbeats of their herds and flocks beat like a war drum, a reminder to sedentary neighbors that the hordes could strike at any moment! For what are such wandering tribes if not armies forever on the march?"*

***"The of the sown lies ripe for any chieftain courageous enough to claim its bounty. Will you lead your tribe beyond the endless expanse and carve out an empire from horseback?"***
