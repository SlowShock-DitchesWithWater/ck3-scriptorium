# Traits — Court type traits


|  | Available only with the Royal Court DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Court type traits can only be obtained by characters that have been courtiers for at least 5 years. For a level 1 trait the court must have reached Grandeur Level 5 while for level 2 the court must have reached Grandeur Level 8. These traits cannot be selected for custom rulers.

| Level 1 trait | Level 1 effects | Level 2 trait | Level 2 effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Diplomatic CourtierThis character has spent at least 5 years living in a bustling Diplomatic Court filled with emissaries and diplomats. | **+1** Diplomacy | Valued Diplomatic CourtierThis character has spent at least 5 years living in a truly grand Diplomatic Court, rubbing elbows with famed emissaries and prominent diplomats. | **+2** Diplomacy **+0.30** Monthly Prestige **+15%** Monthly Diplomacy Lifestyle Experience |
| Warlike CourtierThis character has spent at least 5 years living in a rowdy Warlike Court populated by warriors and tacticians. | **+1** Martial **+1** Prowess | Valued Warlike CourtierThis character has spent at least 5 years living in a truly grand Warlike Court, involving themselves with noble warriors and acclaimed tacticians. | **+2** Martial **+2** Prowess **-5.00%** Men-at-Arms Maintenance **+15%** Monthly Martial Lifestyle Experience |
| Administrative CourtierThis character has spent at least 5 years living in an organized Administrative Court home to many bureaucrats and officials. | **+1** Stewardship | Valued Administrative CourtierThis character has spent at least 5 years living in a truly grand Administrative Court, observing the daily toil of efficient bureaucrats and influential officials. | **+2** Stewardship **+0.20** Monthly Control **+15%** Monthly Stewardship Lifestyle Experience |
| Intrigue CourtierThis character has spent at least 5 years living in a taciturn Intrigue Court hosting smooth-talking envoys and keen-eyed retainers. | **+1** Intrigue | Valued Intrigue CourtierThis character has spent at least 5 years living in a truly grand Intrigue Court, where they have learned to stay on their toes among the many silver-tongued envoys and perceptive retainers. | **+2** Intrigue **+10** Natural Dread **-10.0%** Initial Enemy Hostile Scheme Success Chance **+15%** Monthly Intrigue Lifestyle Experience |
