# Interactions — Courtier Actions


### Ask to Take Vows

Characters following a faith with the  Monasticism tenet or Buddhistic Faiths with the Special Doctrine Immaterial Harmony may request a courtier to take the vows and join the clergy. This interaction costs  **100** piety,  **350** piety if the courtier is important in the line of sucession and has a cooldown of  10 years.

| Requirements | On Accept | On Decline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Faith allows the taking of vows (Monasticism tenet) Courtier shares the same faith Courtier is at least 10 years old Courtier is excommunicated or has already been part of an order Courtier is your Diarch If married or betrothed: Male courtiers must be in a patrilineal marriage Female courtiers must be in a matrilineal marriage | **-25** Legitimacy per primary title rank (doubled if courtier is the primary heir) **+25** Opinion with Court Chaplain if they are close family **+10** Opinion with Head of Faith if they are close family **+2** House Unity if Clan Government and same house Courtier: **-10** Opinion of the requester for 10 years **+1** Faithful Level of Devotion Gains the Devoted trait If married or betrothed, divorces their spouse, breaks the betrothal and loses all concubines | Refunds piety cost from decision **-2** House Unity if Clan Government and same house Courtier: **-15** Opinion of the requester for 10 years |

The accept chance of the interaction is modified by the following (default value is 0):

| Source | Accept Chance |
| --- | --- |
| Weak Hook | **+50** |
| Opinion of the requester | A quarter of the courtier opinion of the influencer |
| Personality | Personality of the courtier |
| Very positive relations with the court chaplain | **+50** |
| Very positive relations with a clergy member | **+50** |
| Virtuous traits | **+20** per virtuous trait |
| Sinful traits | **-20** per sinful trait |
| Holy Warrior trait | **+20** |
| Temperate, Content, Humble | **+25** |
| Ambitious | **-500** |
| Pregnant | **-500** |
| Married or Betrothed | **-50** |
| Importance in line of sucession | **-25** to **-150** |
| Rivalry with the requester | **-50** to **-100** |
| Courtier is intimidated or terrified of the requester | **+25** to **+50** |
