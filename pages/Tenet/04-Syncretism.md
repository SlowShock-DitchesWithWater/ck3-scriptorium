# Tenet — Syncretism


There is a Syncretism tenet for each religion. Syncretism tenets allows characters that have it as core or personal tenet to use artifacts restricted to that religion and gives **+15%** Scheme Success Chance for  Study Rite schemes, which stacks if the tenet is both core and personal. In addition syncretism tenets give the following effects:

- If used as a core tenet the rite borrows at one Virtue with a Virtue Weight of 15 and one or two Sins with a Sin Weight of 10 from another religion, grants **+30** Opinion mutually with all characters and counties following it and makes its faith and the rite with the tenet consider each other Astray/Hostile instead of Hostile/Evil, depending on the Theism doctrine. If a rite has a Syncretism core tenet and attempts to convert a county whose Faith matches it, the County Conversion Speed is reduced by **−75%**. Each rite can have only one Syncretism tenet and cannot have the one matching its own religion.
- If used as a personal tenet all domain counties that follow the tenet's religion gain **+10%** Development Growth and **+10** Popular Opinion. Rulers also unlock the Invite Religious Exiles decision. The syncretism tenet matching the character's own religion cannot be adopted.
