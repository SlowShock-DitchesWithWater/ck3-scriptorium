**When the World Stopped Making Sense (WtWSMS)** is a massive major overhaul for CK3, aiming to allow to play during the Migration Period at any date between 476 (fall of the Western Roman Empire) and 700, and continuing on with that game until the vanilla end date, 1453 (fall of the Eastern Roman Empire).

Compared to other "Dark Ages", Early Middle Ages or Late Antiquity mods, its goal is to model the history of the period in a dynamic and plausible way.

**No DLC is required to play the mod,** although we suggest the following DLC be used for the listed reasons:

- All Under Heaven is needed to unlock Roman Administrative Government mechanics.

## Installation

The mod is available on the Steam Workshop and Paradox Mods, but it can also be downloaded and installed manually.

The download links are accessible via the mod forum thread, in the latest release sticky thread, labelled something like **[WtWSMS] Release x.x.x**.

### Steam Workshop

1. Go to the WtWSMS workshop page
2. Click "Subscribe"
3. Launch CK3
4. Wait for mod download to complete in Steam client
5. Select the mod "WtWSMS" in the Mod tab
6. Play!

### Paradox Mods

1. Go to the WtWSMS Paradox Mods page
2. Click "Add" and select the appropriate playset
3. Launch CK3
4. Wait for mod download to complete in the launcher
5. Select the mod "WtWSMS" in the Mod tab
6. Play!

### Manual

Note that these instructions are written for Windows OS file structure.

1. Remove any WTWSMS mod folders and .mod files inside `Documents\Paradox Interactive\Crusader Kings III\mod`
2. Clear your `gfx` cache, by deleting the folder `Documents\Paradox Interactive\Crusader Kings III\IWTWSMS\gfx`
3. Extract the downloaded archive
4. Enter in the first folder of the extracted file and transfer it's content to `Documents\Paradox Interactive\Crusader Kings III\mod`
5. Launch CK3
6. Select the mod "WtWSMS" in the Mod tab
7. Play!

### Current Features

*Highlights*

- Implemented and extended TypcialCrusader's Tributary System to include Federates
- Utilized the minority system from *Raja's of Asia* for improved granularity with the map
-

#### New Bookmarks

**February 2 AD 476**
  - **Little Augustus** - Odoacer's Revolt and the situation in Italy
  - **Empire in Crises** - The situation in the Eastern Roman Empire
  - **Last Light of the West** - Syagrius' Kingdom in Soissons and the situation in Gaul
  - **Battleground of Cultures** - The growing tensions between the Zoroastrian Persians and Christian Caucasians
  - **Rise of the White Huns** - The rising conflict between the Hepthalites, Rouran, Kidarites, and India

### Planned Features

- New bookmarks and timeline, play between 476 and 800, with all emblematic dates being featured
- Complete redesign of the culture map, dozens of new cultures with accompanying melting-pots
- Overhauled religions, with unique heresies and schisms, ranging from Christian church councils to the Rise of Islam
- A unique government system with transitions and continuities, from Bureaucratic, Gupta and Sassanid rulers to Sub-Roman realms becoming Feudal
- Dynamic decisions and events giving birth to new empires and leading to the implosion of old realms
- Migration mechanics designed dynamically to give choice to players and the AI, with special features for Slavic tribes
- Decaying Roman legions, reconquest mechanics and foederatus contracts
- Flavour events to give a distinct feeling while playing in all regions across the map
- Play in your language of choice - full translations for English, French and Spanish

## Frequency Asked Questions

### Where can I find the latest official version?

In the forum thread, look for the latest release threadmark, labelled something like **Release x.x.x**.

### Does the mod require any DLC?

No. Royal Court is suggested for access to culture mechanics, but no DLC is required.

### Is the mod compatible with all DLCs?

Yes, it is compatible with every DLC (as with any mod that is updated for the latest patch). However, even if it is recommended to run all the DLC's you own with it, the content of said DLC may not be adequate to the new setting of the mod, a consequence of the fact that we are still in alpha.

### Is the mod compatible with any other mod?

See #**Mod Compatibility**

### What languages is the mod available in?

Currently the mod is fully localised in English. Furthermore, there are plans for also providing French (*français*) and Spanish (*español*) localisation in 1.0.0.

### How do I migrate?

Migration requires the player be tribal or nomadic, the year be before AD 800, and have one of the following religions: Germanic Religion, Slavic Religion, or Tengri Religion OR be descended from Sarmatian culture with Sarmatian heritage.
