# Interesting characters — Unlanded characters


Unlanded characters may vary between courts upon starting the game. Although their home may not be pre-defined, these characters have a historical basis and will appear in every game.

**Arnoul de Chiny**: Arnoul is the head of last surviving branch of the legendary Karling linage, decending from King Bernard of Italy. The Chiny branch had never any grand ambitions as minor landowners, Arnould himself would die while traveling for Third Crusade, and his branch would end with his grand daughters not long after.

**Zhiroslav Romanov**. Although Zhiroslav is in no way connected with the boyar family of Mikhail Fedorovich, this does not prevent you from glorifying this family almost 400 years before the fall of the Rurik dynasty.

**Arnfast Frille** and **Torgöt Eketrä**: Two courtiers who serve a similar purpose; the truly last Norse Asatru characters able to spawn. Arnfast seems to spawn in the court of Botulf II Botulfsson of Jåhkåmåhkke, a county in Sápmi. Torgöt, on the other hand, seems to spawn one county down in Ångermanland. They're both consistently 28, Torgöt spawns with a learning education and Arnfast has a diplomacy education. Should one marry them to a female player character, or order them to convert any children in your court, perhaps the old ways of the Norse won't yet die.
