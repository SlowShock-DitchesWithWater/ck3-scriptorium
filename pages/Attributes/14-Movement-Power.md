# Attributes — Movement Power


|  | Available only with the All Under Heaven DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

  - *Main article: Dynastic Cycle#Political movements*

Those who are part of the Dynastic Cycle situation as participants have Personal  Movement Power, which is increased by the following:

- Age
- Title rank
- Merit rank
- Level of devotion
- Level of influence
- Men-at-arms
- Governor trait experience
- Estate upgrades
- Power of disciples
- Being a councilor
- Being a diarch

Every Political Movement has a  Movement Power, which is the sum of Personal Movement Power of all members. Reaching 800 Movement Power unlocks for every member *Dismiss Hook* and *Request Permission to Raid*-interactions, and the Raid Estate scheme.
