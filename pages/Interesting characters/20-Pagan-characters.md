# Interesting characters — Pagan characters


### Slovianskan

#### Lithuania

- **Sareikā Skalauti, Count of Memel**: You are the living embodiment of the Skalvian people, a Baltic tribe closely related to the Prussians. You are also the last landed believer of the long-dead Slavic Pagan beliefs. Surrounding you on all sides are the Vidilists, Baltic pagans who historically will be brutally Christianized in less than 50 years during the Northern Crusades. Can you succeed where so many Pagans before you fell and bring the Slavic people back to their roots, or perhaps stand with the Vidilists against the tide of Christianity threatening to sweep into your homeland?

### Tengri

#### Ötüken

- **Temüjin Borjigin**, **High Chieftain of Ögräsh**: Temüjin was born with a blood clot in his right hand--a sign of extradordinary destiny. Though still young, he has already faced grave trials: the murder of his father, crushing hunger, and imprisonment by rival tribes. Yet his resolve remains unbroken, and now he gather bold riders under his banner. Let the earth quake beneath the thunderous hooves of your endless hordes, and make the world bend to your will!
- **Jamukha Jadaran, Chieftain of Khentii:** A formidable warrior in his own right, Jamukha has navigated boyhood with the aid of Temüjin, and in turn they have formed a bond stronger than those of blood. His innate pride compliments Temüjin's growing taste for power...but the two rarely agree on matters of leadership. Sword of the Altai, shall you subjugate your *anda,* or perish in his shadow?
