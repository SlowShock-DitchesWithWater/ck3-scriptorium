# Interactions — Appointment Actions


### Influence Candidacy

|  | Available only with the Roads to Power DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Adminstrative Government characters can spend  influence to improve or decrease a candidate score, up to their investment capacity, on the selected title as long as they can be appointed to it. Any eligible character can be chosen when interacted directly, but only house members and court members if king or above will show in the recipient list. This interaction has no cooldown.

### Ask to Influence Candidacy

|  | Available only with the Roads to Power DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Adminstrative Government characters can spend 50, up to 300 gold or a  weak hook asking another character to improve a selected eligible candidate score. If the character asked is not a player, they will randomly choose between a minor, medium or major contributon, with a positive opinion and relationship with both the requester and the target increasing the chances of a higher contribution. This interaction has no cooldown.

The acceptance chance of the interaction is modified by the following (default value is -40):

### Retire Governor

|  | Available only with the Roads to Power DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Adminstrative Government independent rulers can force a non-dominant family vassal governor they are not at war with to retire, passing on all of their governorships and landed titles to the next in line. This interaction can not be declined, even with a  strong hook. This interaction has a cooldown of  5 years.

| Target intrigue | **-x2** |
| Target is Craven, Lazy | **+10** |
| Target is Trusting, Generous | **+5** |
