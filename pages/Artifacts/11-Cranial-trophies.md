# Artifacts — Cranial trophies


|  | Available only with the All Under Heaven DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Cranial trophies are trinket artifacts that are obtained by characters whose rite has the core tenet with the same name when they kill other rulers in battle, duel or beheading execution. The rarity and stats of the artifacts depends on the primary title of the executed character.
