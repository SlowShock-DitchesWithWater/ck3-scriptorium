# Interesting characters — Legends of Crusader Kings III


Legends of Crusader Kings III are a set of 5 characters, one for each lifestyle, that have special historical events which may trigger. They are found in the *Rags to Riches* scenario.

- **Murchad ua Briain, Petty King of Munster** - focused on Diplomacy
- **Robert de Hauteville, Duke of Apulia** - focused on Martial
- **Vratislav II Přemyslid, Duke of Bohemia** - focused on Stewardship
- **Yahya Dhunnunid, Emir of Toledo** - focused on Intrigue
- **Matilda di Lucca, Duchess of Tuscany** - focused on Learning and starts with an event dilemma about how to handle her marriage to Gottfried Wigeriche
