# Elder Kings II

**Elder Kings II** is a total conversion for Crusader Kings 3 set within the Elder Scrolls universe. In which a player may take on the role of a variety of different Elder Scrolls races and leaders in order to lead a people or a nation through the turbulent times of the 2nd era, or "interregnum" of the Elder Scrolls universe.

## Development

This mod has been in development since July 2020 and received its first release on November 11, 2022. The current version is 0.9.3.

### Mod Requirements

Generally the latest releases of Elder Kings also require the latest CK3 patches. **Elder Kings does not require DLC**, though there are some additional features that are built upon the **Royal Court** Expansion, more information on that in the FAQ section.

## Setting

Elder Kings II takes place throughout all of Tamriel and Yokuda. Beginning in either 2E 440 or 2E 450, Elder Kings allows players to take control of rulers and shape the history of the continent.

The new 2E 440 "Warlords" Startdate covers the dark period after the fall of the Akaviri Potentate in 2E 430, when the warlord Attrebus starts a war to push the remaining Tsaesci out of Cyrodiil. Meanwhile Valenwood lives through kingless days, due to the recent vanishing of its capital Falinesti. While the descendants of Ar-Azal fight over the title of Hammerfell; and the High Kingdom of Skyrim splits due to the machinations of Svartr.

## Playable Scenarios

### 2E 440: Warlord Rising

It has been ten years since the vile Akaviri Potentate was assassinated by the Morag Tong. Yet some Tsaeschi still cling to the Imperial City, supported by powerful Nibinese nobility in the east. It is time someone disposed of them. The stage is set for the young Attrebus Sossildor - a fanatical follower of the Eight and sworn enemy of Daedra worship and other outlandish beliefs - who has succeeded in uniting the Colovian kingdoms into the Colovian Estates and now stands as Cyrodiil's protector. Most nibenese, however, remain loyal to the Tsaesci, fearful of losing their influence over the Heartlands. Meanwhile, chaos breaks out all over Tamriel as the inevitable Interregnum threatens to shatter what is left of the Empire. Will Attrebus restore what seems lost, or will the "days and nights of blood and venom" reign?

### 2E 450: The Interregnum

Twenty years ago, the last Akaviri Potentate and all of his heirs were murdered, ending the Second Empire. Cyrodiil and High Rock are now split between dozens of rival successor-states. Skyrim struggles with civil unrest as two self-proclaimed High Kings strive to claim the province for themselves. Former subjects of the Empire struggle with one another over even the smallest of resources. Most of Tamriel fares no better, with regional hostilities gripping nearly every region outside of Alinor. In all this chaos, is it possible for the Empire to be restored? Can any individual possibly hope to pacify the turbulence and stand above all the others as the Emperor of not only Cyrodiil but also all of Tamirel? Anything is possible amidst the chaos of the Interregnum.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Mod x work with EK2?

Most likely no. Elder Kings touches almost every vanilla file, meaning any other mod trying to do the same will break. Mods will need to be patched/submods will need to be made. On our Discord you can find a registry of Submods, among them are also compatiblitiy patches.

### Are DLC required to play?

No DLC required. You will however get additional features with DLC enabled, such as the White Gold Tower Throneroom with Royal Court.

We heavily recommend using Royal Court if you plan on playing in Cyrodiil/the Imperial Isle, a pirate government or plan to use the Arcane Court type.

### Is EK2 multiplayer compatible?

We've done some testing and it indeed runs in MP. However, select testing and few mps tried aren't enought to sufficiently come to a conclusion. We'll need feedback from many more players on MP. Issues might come up, fixing them is a priority.

### Will there be more bookmarks?

Yes. However we will only move within our scope of 2E 440 to 2E 896. Any dates outside of that, like the Oblivion Crisis of 3E 433 or the Dragon Crisis of 4E 201, are not planned. Anyone is welcome to make a submod for these however!

### Will missing features from EK1 be brought back?

Yesn't. We consider EK2 more of a sequel than a port. Most content is newly made, not copied over.

However some mechanics of EK1 will make a return in later updates, such as Daedric Interactions.

### Are you taking donations?

No.

## Troubleshooting

**Launching the mod takes ages!**

If this is your first startup, EK2 has a lot of shader/texture calculations to do. These will not have to be done on subsequent launches, meaning only the first startup should take longer than average.

**We encounter desyncs in multiplayer.**

If you encounter them directly after starting a new mp game, consider making a new game in singleplayer, saving it and hosting that savegame in multiplayer. EK2 has many calculations to do at startup, which might lead to issues in mp games.

**There's visual glitches! Beams of light, texture glitching in different colours!**

Go to *\Documents\Paradox Interactive\Crusader Kings III\shadercache* and delete its contents. This will lead the game to regenerate shaders/textures the next time you launch the mod.
