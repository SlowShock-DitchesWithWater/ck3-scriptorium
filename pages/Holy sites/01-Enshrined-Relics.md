# Holy sites — Enshrined Relics


Every holy site can have up to 3 holy relics that require the same faith enshrined or removed either on creation or later by the title holder, top liege, or the owner of the clerical region where the holy site is located. Enshrining and removing holy relics increase or reduce some of the bonuses the holy site gives. Enshrining a holy relic gives **+15**  Opinion with every  Zealot and  Minority vassal as well as the head of faith for 10 years. Removing a holy relic gives **-20**  Opinion with every  Zealot and  Minority vassal as well as the head of faith for 10 years and it also costs 100-500  Piety and Devotion depending on the rarity of the removed holy relic. Holy relics can also be swapped but if the new holy relic is of lower rarity this will still give the penalties.

If the last enshrined holy relic is removed the holy site will become inactive until another holy relic is enshrined. If a holy site it abandoned all enshrined relics will be given to the ruler who holds the country with the former holy site barony. Only the holy site holder, its top liege or the archbishop or patriarch of its clerical region can enshrine or remove artifacts.
