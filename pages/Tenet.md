# Tenet

> [!] This article has been verified for the current PC version (1.20) of the game.

Tenets represent the issues that a rite takes a stance on. Tenets have different statuses within a rite and can also be adopted a personal tenets if the character has personal tenet slots available. Removing a personal tenet gives  Stress and prevents removing or replacing another personal tenet for 3 years. The piety cost to choose a tenet when creating a new rite depends on what religion it belongs to. The cost of the tenet will also be discounted by 50% if it is present in the original rite.

Tenets often have requirements in order to be made Core tenets but adopting a tenet as a personal tenet does not have any requirements.
