# Situation — The Christian Church


The Christian Church is a situation that affects every character whose religion is  Christianity and is located in an existing or potential clerical region. Phases of the situation are called chapters. In 867 the situation starts in the  Fragile Unity chapter, in 1066 it starts in the  Reform chapter, and in 1178 it starts in the  Crusade chapter.

As long as chapter is not  Investiture Controversy heads of rite, cardinals, archbishops, patriarchs, emperors, hegemons and characters whose dynasty finished the  Servants of God legacy can push the situation towards any possible future chapter every 10 years at the cost of 250  Piety. This is 4 times more effective if character is head of faith.

### Before Great Schism

In 867 the situation tracks a value called *Schism Progress*, which ranges from 0 to 13. It is increased by 1 whenever one of the following hapepns:

- An Ecumenical Council activity voted to remove a tenet or doctrine
- A quarrel between two rites happens before the debates of an Ecumenical Council activity
- A war takes place between two Kings or above who are involved in the situation
- A holy site of Roman Rite is converted to Greek Rite or the other way around
- A holy site of Roman Rite or Greek Rite is demoted
- The head of rite of Roman Rite or Greek Rite is murdered by a character who practices the other rite

If any of these happen, next time the situation enters the  Saeculum Obscurum chapter the Great Schism will begin. When the chapter starts all rulers following the faith with most counties will be given the option to stand with either one rite or both and other rulers following the faith with most counties can be asked to change their stance via a new character interaction. If Schism Progress reaches 13 the option to cause or prevent the Great Schism will be unlocked. Both options have the following requirements:

- King or above
- The character or a puppet is head of rite
- 4 of the following:
  - Paragon of Virtue or above
  - Control 1 Holy Site
  - Control 2 Holy Sites
  - Chose to oppose the schism them the Saeculum Obscurum chapter started
  - 7 Other Ecclesiastical rulers following the faith with most counties made the same choice when the chapter started and are either:
    - Puppet
    - Friend or Best Friend
    - Lover or Soulmate
    - Have a hook on them

The Great Schism can end in three ways:

| Cause the Great Schism | Prevent the Great Schism | Saeculum Obscurum chapter ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Situation enters the Concord chapter Dynasty gains the Breakers of Communion modifier Gain *the Schismat* nickname Catholicism is created and Roman Rite, Ambrosian Rite, Insularism and Mozarabism join it Orthodoxy is created and Greek Rite, Georgian Rite and Slavic Rite join it The pope becomes head of faith of Roman Rite The ecumenical patriarch becomes head of faith of Greek Rite Character cannot mend the Great Schism | Situation enters the Concord chapter Dynasty gains the Guardians of Unity modifier Gain *the Concordant* nickname If rite is Roman Rite can make the pope head of faith If rite is Greek Rite can make the ecumenical patriarch head of faith | Situation enters the Reform chapter Catholicism is created and Roman Rite, Ambrosian Rite, Insularism and Mozarabism join it Orthodoxy is created and Greek Rite, Georgian Rite and Slavic Rite join it The pope becomes head of faith of Catholicism The ecumenical patriarch becomes head of faith of Orthodoxy |

Chalcedonianism cannot establish a Head of Faith until the Great Schism happened or is prevented.

### After the Great Schism

In 1066 or 1178 start dates or after the Great Schism has taken place in a 867 start date the  Concord chapter will have the option to mend the Great Schism as long as at least one county follows either a Catholic or an Orthodox rite. Mending the Great Schism has the following effects:

- If faith is Orthodoxy all Catholic rites will become Orthodox rites and if faith is Catholicism all Orthodox rites will become Catholic rites
- If the Great Schism was mended through diplomacy the character's dynasty gains the Peacemakers of Christendom modifier and if the Great Schism was mended through warfare the character's dynasty gains the Restorers of Communion modifier
- Character gains 1500 Prestige
- Character gains 1000 Piety
- Dynasty gains 10000 Renown
- Faith gains 50 Fervor
- Gain *the Ecumenism* nickname
- Rite gains the Ecumenism doctrine if it doesn't have it already
- Every other Christian rite that has the Ecumenism doctrine loses it, loses 25 Fervor, and all its rulers are given the chance to convert
  - AI characters have a 80% chance to convert if they don't have the Zealous trait and will always convert if they have the Cynical trait
- Gain a Holy Legend Seed

This can be done through either diplomacy or warfare.
