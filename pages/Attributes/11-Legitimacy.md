# Attributes — Legitimacy


Legitimacy is a measure of a ruler character's right to rule. Legitimacy can be gained by creating titles (gaining titles through usurpation only decreases the former owner's Legitimacy), releasing higher-ranked prisoners with no demands, taking certain decisions, creating legends, holding court, attending activities, having a Senechal and assigning a Court Musician to the *Bolster Legitimacy* task (effectiveness depends on Aptitude), and winning wars, especially against rulers of higher title rank. These gains can be further modified, positively by having the  Living Legend or  Venerated Ancestor traits, or negatively if the character is a  Bastard or a  Legitimized Bastard.

Legitimacy can be lost by losing titles, accepting faction demands, taking tyrannical actions, discovered hostile schemes, exposed secrets, excommunication, marrying Lowborn characters (only applies to primary spouse but making a Lowborn secondary spouse the primary one applies the penalty as well), plagues starting in the Domain, disinheriting dynasty members, and losing wars, especially civil wars - losses are also magnified for characters with a  Disputed Heritage. When a ruler dies their heirs will inherit part of the Legitimacy depending on how close a relative they were to the previous ruler.

Every ruler starts with a certain amount of Legitimacy. This primarily scales with Dynasty's Level of Splendor, as well as  Court Grandeur if the ruler has a Royal Court. It is also increased by a a high  Diplomacy skill and having the  Just,  Diplomat,  Born in the Purple and  Sayyid traits or traits that are considered virtues and reduced by having the  Inbred trait, traits that are considered sins, or by being a gender that doesn't match the faith's View on Gender doctrine.

Legitimacy is calculated on a scale ranging from 0 to 2800 and goes through 8 levels. How much Legitimacy is required for each level, and how much can be accumulated before excess is wasted, depends on the primary title rank and culture era. Each era will further increase the required Legitimacy for each level by half of the Legitimacy required for level 1. Tribal era values are presented in the table. It should be noted that the title rank starts with  Baron so for example a  Count will need 60 for Aspiring.

Characters with  Administrative,  Celestial,  Meritocratic,  Meritocratic Khanate or  Ritsuryō government will only have Legitimacy if they are independent. Characters with  Ecclesiastical or  Theocratic government do not have Legitimacy.

Rulers with  Nomadic government are expected to have the Rightful legitimacy level and are affected the same way regardless of their title rank. Their legitimacy gains and loses from wars are also doubled and they also gain legitimacy by marrying the noble courtiers of a ruler with equal or higher Dominance law.
