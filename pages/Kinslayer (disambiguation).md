# Kinslayer (disambiguation)

**Kinslayer** may refer to:

- Dynastic Kinslayer, a trait gained when killing dynasty members.
- Familial Kinslayer, a trait gained when killing members of one's extended family.
- Kinslayer (trait), gained when killing close family members.
- Kinslaying (doctrine), a doctrine that determines the legality of killing one's own kin.
