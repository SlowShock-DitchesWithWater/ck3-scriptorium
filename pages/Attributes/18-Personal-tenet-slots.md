# Attributes — Personal tenet slots


Personal tenet slots dictate how many personal tenets a ruler may adopt. It is affected by the following:Every character can adopt one personal tenet, modified by the following:

- **+1** Paragon of Virtue level of devotion or above
- **+1** Radiant lifestyle perk
- **+1** Zealous trait
- **+1** Wise Man/Woman trait if rite has Gnosticism
- **+1** Godlike level of devotion
- **+1** Third Servants of God dynasty legacy
- **+1** Blood of New Jerusalem modifier
- **+1** Puppeted Toward Salvation modifier and member of the clergy
- **+1** Completed a Grand Cathedral great project within the last 10 years
- **-1** Cynical trait
- **-1** Sinner level of devotion

Adopted personal tenets are not removed if the character's personal tenet slots decreases.
