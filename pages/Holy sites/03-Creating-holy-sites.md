# Holy sites — Creating holy sites


|  | Available only with the By God Alone DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Rulers who meet the requirements to change the status of holy sites can also create new ones in baronies in their realm that no other faith considers a holy site. This costs 2000  Piety and requires a total artifact rarity of at least 3 among all enshrined holy relics. There are 10 types of holy sites and a faith can only have one of each.
