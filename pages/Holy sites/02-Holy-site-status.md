# Holy sites — Holy site status


Holy sites have one of two statuses. Changing the status of a holy site costs 1000  Piety and can only be done every 5 years.

- **Regular holy sites** provide a bonus to their holder, a bonus to their county that scales with holy relic rarity, can be used for Pilgrimage activities by any character who follows the same faith, allow the construction of a holy building by any ruler in de jure hierarchy who follows the same faith, and are necessary to create a Head of Faith title or to reform an Unreformed faith. Turning an eminent holy site into a regular holy site gives **-20** Opinion with every Zealot and Parochial vassal of the same faith for 10 years and if the holy site had an owner it also gives **-50** Opinion with them for 10 years.
- **Eminent holy sites** provide all the bonuses of regular holy sites and also provide a bonus to all character of their faith. Each faith can only have 3 eminent holy sites. Turning a regular holy site into an eminent holy site gives **+30** Opinion with every Zealot vassal of the same faith for 10 years and if the holy site had an owner it also gives **+50** Opinion with them for 10 years.
