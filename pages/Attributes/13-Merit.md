# Attributes — Merit


The Merit of a character who passed all examinations

|  | Available only with the All Under Heaven DLC enabled. |
| --- | --- |

Merit replaces  Legitimacy for vassals with certain governments. Every such vassal has a **Career Path** and a **Merit Rank**. If their liege has the same government the vassal can only be given titles if their Merit Rank is high enough and if a character dies their titles and any council position will automatically be given to the character who has the same Career Path and highest Merit Rank although the liege can revoke them at any point. A player can change their Career Path via a decision unless they are landed or a councilor.

Vassals who have Merit may have Elder-Disciple relationships with each other, their courtiers or their liege. The Elder must be either the liege or a character with a higher Merit Rank than the liege and the relationship ends if none of these conditions are valid anymore. If both characters are involved in the Dynastic Cycle situation and they are not part of the same political movement the Disciple will have to join the Elder's political movement or pay 100  Influence and 20  Opinion with the Elder. Each character can have up to 5 Disciples but the limit can be increased. Elders and disciples can ask each other as well as other disciples of the same elder for  Gold,  Candidate Score, an artifact or a men-at-arms regiment. If the character asking is part of a Political Movement they may also ask for a knight, feast, or courtier with the  Confucian Education trait. If the request is accepted the character will gain between 10 and 160  Merit depending on the primary title rank of the asked character.

### Merit Rank

The maximum Merit Rank a character can reach is limited by their currently-passed exams. Passing an exam requires attending another character's examination activity with the  Take Examination intent and succeeding.

- **Provincial Examination** is passed via the Local Examination activity and increases the maximum Merit Rank by **+2** and increases the Exam Score of the following examinations
- **Metropolitan** Examination is passed via the Capital Examination activity and increases the maximum Merit Rank by **+2**
- **Palace Examination** is passed via the Capital Examination activity by being among the top 10 characters by Exam Score and increases the maximum Merit Rank by **+3**
