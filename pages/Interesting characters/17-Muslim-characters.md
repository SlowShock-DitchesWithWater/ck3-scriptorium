# Interesting characters — Muslim characters


### Ash'ari

#### Egypt

- **Salāh al-Dīn Yusuf Ayyubid, King of Egypt:** The last vizier of the Fatimids has thus far proven himself an energetic and capable successor to the dynasty he overthrew, despite a recent defeat at the hands of the leprous young King of Jerusalem. Though formally invested by the Abbasid Caliph as Sultan of Egypt and Syria, as long as the crusaders' descendants stubbornly cling to their ancestors' conquests, his kingdom will never be truly complete. Can the Ayyubids triumph over the kingdom of Jerusalem to reclaim the Holy Land, or will this new dyansty's fall come as suddently as its rise?

#### Jazira

- **Muzaffar** **al-Dīn Gököri Begteginid, Count of Harran:** Deprived of his patrimony and banished into exile in favor of a younger brother, Gököri lent his blade and bravery to a Zengid prince and won himself a new fief in Harran. His service to his new liege has been constant, bu the prince he serves lies stricken by an illness he is unlikely to survive, and Gököri kows well that loyalty must have its limits, and an uncertain future often heralds opportunties. The Sultan of Egypt has proven just how far a man of will and ability can rise: can you follow in his footsteps and found a new dyansty of your own?

#### Kabulistan

- **Ghiyath al-Din Muhammad II, King of Kabulistan:** Play the zealous Islamic Shah, Start by taking down the Ghaznavid then you can go for your conquest of India. Can you establish the Sultanate of Delhi?

### Maturidi

#### Rum

- **Kilij Arsland, Sultan of Rum:** Fresh from victory over the Byzantines at the battle of Myriokephalon, Kilij Arsland has proven himself a power to be reckoned with in Anatolia. Given time, he may march his armies to the very walls of Constantinople itself, but the jockeying of his many sons for the succession could prove a deadly distraction, for even Kilij Arslan's own father ascended the throne over the body of his own brother. Who among your sons will proven worthy to carry on your endeavors, and can can Rum survive their rivalries?

#### Transoxiana

- **Alā al-Dīn Tekish Khwarezmid, Sultan of the Khwarezmid Sultanate:** With the decline of the Seljuks, his realm is on the rise. Just don't make the same mistake as Tekish's descendent and execute the Great Khan's envoys.

### Nizari

#### Khorasan

- **Nur al-Dīn Muhammed II Nizarid, Imam of the Nizari Imamate:** Historically, Muhammed was able to manage the Nizari Ismaili state of mountain fortresses from the relative saftey of Alamut. Alas, the in-game Imam has no such protection and his small territory will almost immediately be swallowed up by the hostile Sunnis surrounding him. To preserve meager demesne by swearing allegence to the neighboring Seljuks or Ghurids. Alternatively, you could allow yourself to be conqurered and go out wandering as a landless adventurer and gather money and soldiers to reestablished the Imamate in a more secure location.

#### Syria

- **Rashīd al-Dīn Sinan Abdel Nour, Hashshashinshah of the Abdel Nour Shahdom:** The famous "Old Man of the Moutains" who led the Syrian Nizaris from Maysaf, Sinan faces a predicament similar to his Imam in Khorasan, in addition to having zero legitimacy. Can you overcome these odds, and like the real Sinan, turn yourself into legend.
