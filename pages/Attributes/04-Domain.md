# Attributes — Domain


The **Domain** is the collection of all holdings a ruler owns personally. Each ruler has a **Domain Limit**; this determines how many holdings from which they can efficiently collect  Taxes and  Levies. Once the Domain Limit is reached, newly controlled holdings will stop providing  taxes and  levies after a year passes.

For rulers with  Feudal,  Clan,  Tribal,  Administrative,  Celestial,  Meritocratic and  Meritocratic Khanate government the Domain Limit is decided in the game rule with the same name and increased by the following:
