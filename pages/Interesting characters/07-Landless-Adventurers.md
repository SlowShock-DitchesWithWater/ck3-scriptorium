# Interesting characters — Landless Adventurers


If you have the Roads to Power DLC, you will have access to Landless Adventurers. Adventurers are playable landless noble characters who travel the world with followers and a Camp, and have a different set of lifestyle perks. As they have no steady income from landed titles, adventurers primarily earn gold by completing contracts for landed rulers, and must stock up on provisions in order to move the camp.

- **Hrolfr de Normandie, of the Walkers from the North**: A famous Viking warrior and the great-great-great-grandfather of William *the Conqueror*. He is only a landless adventurer if you have the DLC, otherwise he is an unlanded character. Historically, he settled in Normandy and founded the dynasty that would one day take England's throne. The character Rollo in the History Channel television show *Vikings* is based on Hrolfr.
- **Ubbe Ragnarrsson Ubbing, of the Scourge of the Saxons**: A renowned Viking warrior and son of the legendary Ragnarr Loðbrók, Ubbe was one of the leaders of the Great Heathen Army alongside his brothers. Historically, he may have had some ties to Frisia where he was apparently considered a duke. Will you stay in England to rule alongside your brothers Halfdan and Ivar, or will you head to Frisia and carve out your own path? The choice is yours.
- **Anangapida Karkota, of Lalitāditya's Successors**: The last living member of the great Kardota dynasty, whose throne was recently usurped by the Utpala dynasty. He has claims on oth the Kingdoms of Kashmir and Punjab and a burning desire to reclaim his ancestral lands. However at 79 years old, this task is challenging to say the least. Can you produce an heir capable of restoring the imperial throne of Rajasthan to the Karkota dynasty, or will Anangapida die forgotten by history? The fate of this ancient dynasty lies in your hands.
- **Enian Yaglaqar, of the Scattered Lights**: The last Khagan of the Uyghur Khaganate, Enian is in exile after losing all his lands and titles. Wandering with his son in the lands of the Kirghiz Khanate, he follows the Manichaean faith. Will you reclaim the Uyghur people's lost lands and re-establish their empire, or will you lead your people in their migration to the south, reuniting with them to secure their future? The fate of the Uyghur people depends on you.
- **Husayn al-Ahwāzī, of the Transmitters of Truth**: Husayn, a devout follower of Imami Shia Islam, is renowned in the Shia world for his hadith collections and jurisprudential works. Wandering the hostile Sunni-dominated Abbasid Caliphate, can you spread the Imami faith and overthrow the Caliph to guide the Arabs to the true Islam, or will you die a martyr like your historical counterpart?
- **Suleyman Qutid, of the Regents of Sabta**: A descendant of Count Julian of Ceuta and Princess Faldrina, daughter of the Visigothic King Egica. Not much is known about Suleyman, but his lineage ties him to the ancient kings of the Visigoths. Will you restore this legacy, overthrow the Umayyads, and trade your Muwalladi faith for the Mozarabic faith of your ancestor Julian and restore the councils of Toledo? Alternatively, you can remain Muwalladi, become the Caliph of the Iberian Muslims and expel the Berbers and Bedouins from Spain to establish Visigothic rule over Iberia.
- **Ali ibn Muhammad Fatimid, of the Rightly Guided Sons**: A member of the legendary Fatimid dynasty, Ali directly descends from Fatima, the daughter of the Prophet Muhammad. As the tenth Imam of the Twelver Shia tradition, Ali wanders the Arabian Desert. Will you establish the Imami faith in southern Arabia and gather a following to rival the Abbasids for leadership of the Ummah?
- **Hussayn ibn Ahmed Fatimid, of the Followers of Isma'il**: A member of the legendary Fatimid dynasty, Hussayn was originally available as an unlanded courtier before the Roads to Power DLC. Now, he appears as a landless adventurer near the Persian Gulf. A devout follower of Isma'ili Islam and the 10th Isma'ili Imam, he is best known as the father of the first Fatimid Caliph and for laying the groundwork for the dynasty's rise to power in North Africa. Can you surpass his legacy, founding the Fatimid Caliphate a generation earlier and challenging Abbasid rule to spread Isma'ili Islam across the Arab world?
- **Guttorm Sigurðrsson Hjort, of the Serpent's Banner**: The maternal uncle of Harald Fairhair, the first King of Norway, Guttorm played a key role in his nephew's rise to power, earning lands, titles, and wealth for his loyalty. Will you follow in his historical footsteps to help crown Harald as king, or will you forge your own path by uniting the Norwegian tribes to become their first king yourself? Alternatively, you can remain in Denmark and overthrow Sigurðr Snake-in-the-Eye, laying the foundations to conquer all of Scandinavia. Note: he is also treated as being the same person as Guthrum, the first Norse king of East Anglia.
- **Ziyar Ziyarid, of the Heirs of Āgoš Vehādān**: The founder of the Zoroastrian Ziyarid dynasty, Ziyar faces a hostile world dominated by relentless Islamisation. Can you surpass the historical Ziyarids, who briefly ruled parts of Western and Northern Persia, by establishing a lasting Zoroastrian kingdom? Will you restore the ancient faith to all of Persia and drive the Arab invaders back to their lands? The fate of Persia and its ancient traditions rests upon your shoulders.
- **Zhū Quánzhōng, of the Iron Host of Zhu:** Would go on to join the Huáng Chāo Rebellion but ended up surrendering to the Tang government. He was given land by the Huángdì and became a political and military mastermind. He encouraged Emperor Ai (Li Zuo) to abdicate in favour of himself in 907 and founded the Liang dynasty (also known as Later Liang), becoming the first emperor of the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period. If you're up for a challenge, can you obtain land and find a way to elevate Zhū Quánzhōng to Huángdì?
