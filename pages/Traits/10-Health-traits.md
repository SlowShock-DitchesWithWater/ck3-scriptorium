# Traits — Health traits


A Health trait is a negative trait that lowers a character's  Health and is visible on the character. They last a varying amount of time and differ greatly in lethality.

The visual effect of certain health traits can be replaced with one from a lesser trait by disabling *Show graphic diseases and injuries* option in the settings menu.

### Wounds

There are three traits of increasing severity that represents a character's current level of wounds. They are increased as part of various events, hunts, duels, or battles, and have a chance to be reduced every 3 months. The chance to reduce wounds decreases with the character's age and  Stress. They cannot be added to custom characters. If a character gets wounded while they already have the highest level of wounds they will die. When a character fully recovers from wounds they have a 40% chance to gain or increase the  Scarred trait.

Each time a character's wounds increase, there is a 10% chance that 2 or 3 months later the wound will become infected, giving a severe  Health Penalty. 3 months later there is a 15% chance the infection will go away and a 10% chance the infection will be replaced by gangrene, giving an even more severe  Health Penalty.

### Diseases

Diseases are Health traits that can be spread from one character to another. If a character recovers from a disease they become immune to it. Immunity is permanent for the Bubonic Plague, Smallpox and Measles and lasts 20 years for other traits. There is no immunity against the Ill trait. STD diseases are congenital and can also be spread to other characters if sleeping with them.

Each year a character has a 10% chance to gain a Diseases trait, though this can be reduced in multiple ways. Diseases are also gained from plagues being in the domain or travel baronies. The Bubonic Plague may or may not appear depending on game rules.

The health penalties can be reduced or canceled if a  Personal Physician with sufficient Aptitude was employed.

### Old age traits

Old age traits are added to characters who are elderly enough, with the chance increasing the older the character gets. These traits progress through 4 levels but progression can be slowed down. Each trait level adds to the previous ones. Which traits are available depends on the Elderly Health game rule but can be added to custom characters regardless, where they cost **-20** customization points. They cannot be obtained by rulers who are immortal.

### Incapable

Incapable is the most severe health trait and cannot be added at character creation. It prevents the character from having children, declaring war, performing schemes, hosting or joining activities, being councilors or knights, or taking part in duels, forces them into an Entrenched Regency, all their skills will always be 0, and if the character has a royal court the throne will be empty. It also prevents them from making decisions except for the newly-unlocked Attempt Suicide decision and gives the following additional penalties:

- **-25** Natural Dread
- **+100%** Dread Loss
- **Severe Health Penalty**
