# Attributes — Spiritual fulfillment


Spiritual fulfillment is a measure of how at peace a character feels in respect to their faith. The baseline is calculated based on the traits the character has and whether they are considered virtues or sins. Spiritual fulfillment can be gained or lost by acting in ways that the faith considers appropriate, and Personal Tenets can be adopted to offset harmful traits. Spiritual fulfillment ranges from **-100** to **100**.
