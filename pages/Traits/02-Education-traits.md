# Traits — Education traits


When reaching adulthood every character receives an education trait based on how their education focus went and can be upgraded by completing  University Visit activities. Level 5 education traits can only be obtained if the child's dynasty finished the  Legitimacy dynasty legacy or the character has an estate with the  Book Collection or  Study Room upgrade.

For custom rulers level 1 traits are free, level 2 traits cost **20**, level 3 traits cost **40**, level 4 traits cost **80** and level 5 traits cost **150**.
